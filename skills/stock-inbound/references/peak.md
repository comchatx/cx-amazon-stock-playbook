# Peak: arrival dates and the season, as rules

## The dates

- Amazon's peak dates are arrival dates at the fulfilment centre, not ship dates (I24).
- Since 2025 they differ by event and by placement option, one per option, and two options can share a date; no order between the options holds for every event (I24).
- Amazon posts them weeks to months ahead, for Prime Day, Prime Big Deal Days and Black Friday Week with Cyber Monday (I24).
- UK and EU dates are published separately, and Europe's AWD post names both an arrival date and a date to create the replenishment by (I24).
- No operation returns a cut-off (I24).

## The season

- Amazon keeps receiving after a peak date but says a late arrival "isn't guaranteed to be processed in time" for Prime eligibility (I25).
- Amazon says its centres focus on receiving in the months before the holiday peak and on customer orders during it, when capacity limits may be lower and inbound slots limited, in the US and Europe; the months are in that year's holiday post (I25).
- Meltable stock has a closed inbound season in US and Canadian fulfilment centres whose dates Amazon sets each year in its meltable posts on the store's seller forum, and AWD takes no meltables (G16).
