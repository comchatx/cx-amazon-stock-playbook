# What only Seller Central shows

The rest of what only Seller Central shows sits with the skill whose job it serves: ../../stock-cost/SKILL.md, ../../restock/SKILL.md, ../../stock-inbound/SKILL.md, ../../stuck-stock/SKILL.md and ../../fbm-and-channels/SKILL.md.

## Inventory Performance Index

- No SP-API operation, report or Data Kiosk dataset returns an account's Inventory Performance Index score, its parts as account measures, or its threshold (Q1).
- The score is on Seller Central's Inventory Performance dashboard; whether the dashboard shows a current numeric threshold was read in Amazon's documents and not yet seen on a live store (Q1).
- The planning report's `sell-through` and `estimated-excess-quantity` are per-SKU figures, not the account's score (Q1).

## Capacity limit

- The FBA capacity limit and its estimates are in Seller Central's Capacity Monitor, and no operation or reachable page gives them (Q2, W45).
- Amazon's US texts disagree on which week of the month the monthly capacity limit is announced and on how many months of estimates follow (Q2).
- One side is Amazon's 2023 announcement; the other is help page GAFNWEYTJUV2GBFC as the listing seat read it on 2026-10-14, with a 2026 staff post (Q2).
- The help page that states the rule is one this research could not read (Q2).

## Remeasurement

- A remeasurement is requested through a Seller Central widget, Amazon states a monthly limit on requests, and no API files or tracks one (Q3).

## AWD

- Amazon's AWD Inventory report, which shows AWD and FBA stock together, is a Seller Central download; AWD's auto-replenishment setting and blocked-SKU list are in no model, report or release note (W39).

## The Agent Policy

- Amazon's notice of 2026-02-17 says that from 2026-03-04 the Business Solutions Agreement carries an Agent Policy with "requirements for the use of automated software or AI agents to access Amazon Services" (Q4).
- The notice says all AI agents "Clearly identify themselves as automated systems", comply with the Agent Policy and "Cease access if Amazon requests", and that Amazon "may restrict their access" (Q4).
- The agreement's and the policy's own text were not read (Q4).
