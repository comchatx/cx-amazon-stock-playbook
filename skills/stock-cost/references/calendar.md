# The dated fee calendar

## 2020–2022

- 2020, UK and EU; 2021-01-01 and 2022-01-01, US: numeric IPI thresholds took effect under the quarterly storage-limit system (C28).

## 2023

- 2023-03-01, US and Europe: one monthly capacity limit per storage type replaced the quarterly storage limits (C24, C28).
- 2023-04-01, US: the storage utilization surcharge began (C4).
- 2023-05-01, Europe: the storage utilization surcharge began (C4).
- 2023-05-01, Canada: the monthly capacity limit began (C24).
- 2023-09-01, Canada: the storage utilization surcharge began (C4).
- 2023-12-19: Amazon's News post defined historical days of supply at the parent-product level (C12).
- 2023-12-26, Japan: Amazon's News post announced the storage utilization surcharge (C4).

## 2024

- 2024-03-01, US: the inbound placement service fee began (C29).
- 2024-04-01, US: the low-inventory-level fee began on standard-size units at the parent-product grain, the first month credited (C11, C16).
- 2024-04-18, Japan: Amazon's News post stated the low-inventory-level fee's threshold (C17).
- 2024-05-15, US: products below a weekly unit floor became exempt from the low-inventory-level fee (C16).
- 2024-06-01, Japan: the storage utilization surcharge began (C4).
- 2024-07-02: Amazon's changelog correction on the Economics dataset's role was created (C40).
- 2024-10-01, Japan: monthly capacity limits for all sellers began and the storage overage fee was abolished (C24, C27).
- 2024-12-04, US: Amazon posted AWD fee changes (C20).

## 2025

- 2025-02-20, US: partial splits became unavailable for standard-size products (C29).
- 2025-05-15, Mexico: the pricing page marks its storage and prolonged-storage rates updated (C44).

## 2026

- 2026-01-15, US: the low-inventory-level fee moved to FNSKU grain, Small and Large Bulky added and Grocery exempt, by help pages as the listing playbook's research read them on 2026-10-14 (C16).
- 2026-01-16, US: the aged-surcharge schedule in force took effect (S12, C7).
- 2026-01-29, US: Amazon's article on per-unit removal and disposal fees, its forum post revised on 2026-02-16 (C31).
- 2026-03-01, US: removal and disposal fees charged per unit as processed, for orders created from this date (C31).
- 2026-05-01, UK: removal and disposal fees charged per unit as processed (C31).
- 2026-10-28: Amazon's News post on the New Selection exemption (C18).
- 2026-11-02, Europe: the rate card edition read here took effect (C1, C34).
- 2026-11-05, US and Canada: Amazon announced the 2027 changes to storage, the aged surcharge and the low-inventory-level fee (C14, C21).
- 2026-11-10: no 2027 storage change announced for the UK and Europe, Japan or Mexico (C23).

## Every year

- The peak fulfilment fee period is posted each year in the store's holiday post; it was the same in 2023–24 and 2026–27 (C35).
- The New Selection terms are posted each year on the store's seller forum (C18).
