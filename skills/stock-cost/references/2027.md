# The 2027 US and Canadian change

- The dated history before it: references/calendar.md.

## Storage

- Amazon announced on 2026-11-05, in *Update to US Referral and Fulfillment by Amazon fees for 2027*, that from 2027-02-01 the US removes the storage utilization surcharge and folds the aged inventory surcharge into one age-based monthly storage rate (C21).
- Canada announced the same design in its News posts (C21).
- Amazon's letter says the design gives flat or lower fees for inventory that turns quickly and higher fees for aged inventory (C21).
- The effective date and the end of the utilization surcharge are in the letter, https://sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com/update-to-us-referral-and-fulfillment-by-amazon-fees-for-2027, and in Amazon's Global Selling notice, https://globalselling.amazon.com/news/news-notics-261105 (C21, C4).
- As of 2026-11-10 the 2027 storage rates and age bands are in no Amazon text readable without sign-in: help page GVZX6EAEL2KZ3SGA needs sign-in (C22).
- The only table found is Seller Essentials', so a rate from it is that party's report (C22).
- Once the change is in force, the rate applied is on the storage fee report's rate columns (C22, C41).
- Which columns of the storage, long-term storage and planning reports carry the new rate is in no Amazon document read and not yet seen on a live store (C21, C10).
- As of 2026-11-10 Amazon had announced no 2027 change to storage charges for the UK and Europe, Japan or Mexico; the design is a US and Canadian fact (C23).

## The low-inventory-level fee

- The same letter of 2026-11-05 says the US fee will apply only when inventory falls below the stated days of supply, measured on actual shipments both in a window before and in a window after the assessment date; Canada announced the same sentence (C14).
- The threshold and the windows are in the letter and the Global Selling notice (C14).
- Under it, weekly per FNSKU, the inventory recorded on the assessment date is divided by average daily shipments in the window before, and again, once the window after has passed, by those in the window after (C14).
- The fee is charged only if both figures are under the threshold, and only after the second computation, so in arrears by that window (C14).
- Today's rule, by Amazon's letter, assesses the fee on trailing sales (C12, C14).
- Whether a week was charged is the planning report's fee flags and Finances (C14).
- A third party, PPC Land (2026-11-06), relays from Amazon's 2027 detail page what none of Amazon's public texts read states: inbound units do not count toward the inventory, MCF and Remote Fulfillment shipments count toward demand and seller-fulfilled shipments do not (C15).
- By the same relay the rate follows the higher of the two figures, the fee is charged only on units shipped in the week after the check, all oversize tiers enter, and the fee gets a standalone line and a new report (C15).
- That detail page is help page GFU8NTVGHP2JQLCR, which this research could not read (C15).
- Amazon's Global Selling notice on the 2027 fee suggests keeping inventory at a level beside the fee's boundary; that is Amazon's suggestion, not a term of the fee (C42).

## AWD

- Amazon's Global Selling notice states that US AWD storage is charged by age from a date in 2027, older stock at a multiple of the standard rate; the rates and the date are in the notice (C20).
