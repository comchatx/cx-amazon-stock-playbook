# Unfulfillable stock

- How a call is made and a report is asked for and landed: the amazon-sp-api skill's references/door.md.

## Customer damaged and repackaging

- "Customer damaged" means a return that cannot be resold as new, which includes an opened package (S13).
- Amazon does not reimburse a customer-damaged unit, and the unit stays unfulfillable until removed (S13).
- Amazon repackages eligible returns automatically and the seller cannot turn that off (S13).
- Refurbishment is optional (S13).
- The returns report shows "Pending Repackaging" and "Repackaged Successfully" (S13).

## What Amazon does with it

- In the US, from 2023-11-01, unfulfillable stock with no return option set is disposed of at Amazon's discretion, including immediately after a return is evaluated (S14).
- In the US, keeping a removal schedule requires the return option and a valid address in the Automated unfulfillable settings (S14).
- In the UK and the EU, from 2024-09-16, unfulfillable stock with no automated setting is disposed of or donated after a notice period, and that disposal order cannot be cancelled (S14).
- The rules and the notice period are in the store's News posts on the seller forum (US 2023-09-08; EU 2024-09-03) (S14).
- What was disposed is in the removal order detail report (S14).

## Expiry

- Amazon removes expiry-dated units ahead of the date and does not return units marked for disposal; the rule and its figures: ../stock-position/SKILL.md (G17).
