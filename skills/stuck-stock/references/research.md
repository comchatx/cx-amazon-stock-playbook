# Markdown and returns research

- In the base model of markdown pricing the purchase cost is sunk: the price of stock already owned depends on units left, time left and the salvage value (Gallego and van Ryzin 1994; theory) (S28).
- Measured gains from model-based markdowns over managers' judgement are small against clearance revenue, from store apparel in 1993–2008, with one controlled experiment (Caro and Gallien 2012; Heching et al. 2002; Caro and Sáez de Tejada Cuenca 2023) (S29).
- The error those studies measured in managers' markdowns was markdowns taken late and deep (S29).
- No study was found on Amazon sellers' excess stock, storage-fee incentives or liquidation recovery (S29).
- Returned goods lost value while waiting, at a weekly rate for inkjet printers and a monthly rate for power tools, in two manufacturers' data of about 1999–2004 (Guide et al. 2006) (S30).
