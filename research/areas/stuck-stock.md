## Amazon's own rules

### A. Stranded inventory

A1. **Definition.** "Stranded inventory is FBA inventory in fulfillment centers that does not have an associated active offer and thus is not available for sale on Amazon. When inventory is stranded, it requires either reactivating the listing or removing inventory." [official, forum staff] (Bryce_Amazon, "Resolving Stranded FBA inventory", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f77cbf98-0f2f-4834-8d3f-9eb9cfe84c8e, posted 2024-03-12, read 2026-11-10). The same sentence, without "thus", is on the help page *Fix stranded inventory* (G201436600, version 19) [official, read 2026-10-14 by the listing playbook's seat].

A2. **Causes Amazon names.** "When you delete or close a listing, or change it from Fulfilled by Amazon to Fulfilled by Merchant, it might strand your inventory. Product listing issues, infringement concerns, or system errors can also result in stranded inventory." [official, forum staff] (Bryce_Amazon, same post). The help page adds "the listing is suppressed or was never created" [official, read 2026-10-14 by the listing playbook's seat].

A3. **How Amazon tells the seller.** "Amazon will notify you about stranded inventory in three ways: By email; On Fix Stranded Inventory; Under Fix stranded inventory in the Notification section of your Inventory Dashboard." [official, forum staff] (Bryce_Amazon, same post). No SP-API notification type carries a stranded event (section F7).

A4. **The reason decides the fix, and the reason table is in Seller Central.** "Resolving stranded inventory depends entirely on the reason the inventory was stranded in the first place. This page in Seller Central has a comprehensive table that includes the various stranded inventory reason codes, and the additional information you will need to resolve it." [official, forum staff] (Bryce_Amazon, same post). The table is on *Resolve stranded inventory issues* (GEYJTVJPWRYUTADQ), which this seat could not open. A search summary of that page says the Fix stranded inventory page has a "Stranded reason" column and an "Additional information" column, and that Fix stranded inventory "has the most accurate and up-to-date information" when the two disagree [snippet]. **The full list of Amazon's stranded reasons was not read first-hand.** What was read:

| Reason, as named | Who names it | What is said about it |
|---|---|---|
| Listing closed | [official] News_Amazon 2019-06-25 (A5) | Relisted automatically |
| Listing closed and inventory error | [official] same | Relisted automatically |
| Item discontinued | [official] same | Relisted automatically |
| Merchant fulfilled (the offer was changed to seller-fulfilled) | [official] same | Changed back to FBA automatically |
| Potential high pricing error | [official, forum staff] Manny_Amazon 2025-10-11 (A9) | Price above the seller's own maximum, above Amazon's validation ceiling, or flagged under the Marketplace Fair Pricing Policy |
| Listing error | [official, forum staff] Bryce_Amazon acknowledged a wave of "Listing Error" strandings as a technical issue [snippet]; [opinion] Jungle Scout (P1) | Units tied to no active ASIN |
| Pricing error, Restricted ASIN, Suspended listing, Brand qualification required, Expired product | [opinion] Jungle Scout (P1) | Jungle Scout's list of "the most common" |

The task's other candidate reasons ("listing deleted", "incomplete listing", "search suppressed", "quality or safety hold", "account-level block", "at risk of stranded") were not found in any Amazon text opened in this session. They are open (O1).

A4a (gap fill 2026-11-10). **Account health as a cause.** The help page *Inventory Buyability* (GFC63UNUF6LMLK2R, version 1) defines stranded units as "in fulfillment centers but associated with a product that doesn't have an active offer. This is usually due to a listing or account health issue." [official, read 2026-10-14 by the listing playbook's seat] (`packages/cx-listing-playbook/research/angles/inventory-and-fulfillment.md` B1, re-read by the verifier on 2026-11-10). This is the only Amazon sentence found that names account health as a cause; it does not name a reason code for it.

A5. **Automatic fixes (since 2019-06-25).** "Changing fulfillment channels and relisting are now automated on Fix stranded inventory. … Now when listings are approved by Amazon, they become automatically available for customers within 24 hours. You can also now set the time (up to 30 days) that inventory is stranded by clicking Edit automatic-action settings on Fix stranded inventory. The new automated settings are set for a default of 30 days. Automatic relist relists inventory that has been stranded under these stranded reasons: Listing closed; Listing closed and inventory error; Item discontinued. Automatic change to FBA changes the fulfillment channel to FBA for inventory that has been stranded under the stranded reason Merchant fulfilled. For listings that you don't want automatically relisted or changed to FBA, select Opt out of auto action … You can also avoid automatic reactivation by setting the Start selling date/Offer start date to a future time." [official] (News_Amazon, "New: Automated fixes for stranded inventory", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/25f239badad36e7e51dfc757a840a2c6, posted 2019-06-25, read 2026-11-10). A search summary of the current help page says the change to FBA "happens automatically after five days" for seller-fulfilled stranded units and is adjustable in the same settings [snippet]. The 2019 default (30 days) and the 2026 summary (five days) differ; the live value is the seller's setting, visible only in Seller Central (S1).

A6. **Aged stranded stock is removed, not reclassified (since 2020-06-30).** "Beginning June 30, 2020, FBA aged stranded inventory will no longer be classified as unsellable. Instead, such inventory will be automatically removed from Amazon fulfillment centers based on seller-configured settings. … you can select whether you'd like your stranded inventory returned to you or disposed of, and set the number of days before automatic removal." [official] (News_Amazon, "Update to FBA stranded inventory removals", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/3e3c67d7231c0cf7d76db8961ad10da8, posted 2020-07-02, read 2026-11-10).

A7. **How many days, and what happens with no setting: Amazon's texts differ.**
- "If your inventory remains stranded, you receive an email notification from Amazon. 30 days after this notification, your inventory will be automatically removed. If you have designated Amazon to accelerate the removal of stranded inventory, the units will be removed more quickly." [official, forum staff] (Bryce_Amazon, 2024-03-12, same post as A1).
- "If you have not configured your stranded removal settings, your inventory will be disposed after maximum days allowed for the stranded reason code." "Stranded inventory units with active offers will not be automatically removed." [official, read 2026-10-14 by the listing playbook's seat] (*Stranded inventory required removals*, GKQ8DGGU529BJJ7E, version 14).
- A 2024 Amazon notice quoted in a forum thread: with no settings chosen on Fix stranded inventory, "some or all your inventory will be disposed of after 90 days from the date of this notification" [snippet].
- A search summary of the current help page: "All sellers are expected to update their stranded auto-removal settings", and a seller with several stores "must configure your auto-removal settings in each store" [snippet].

So: the number of days is per stranded reason code, capped by Amazon, shortened by the seller's setting; with no setting the outcome is disposal. The dated value for a given unit is in the stranded report's `Date-to-take-auto-removal` column (A11), not in any rule this file can state.

A8. **UK and EU are separate from each other.** Since 2021-05-06 a seller can set "a local return address for inventory stored in the UK, and a separate local return address for inventory stored in EU countries", or dispose in one and return in the other. "Remember to check both UK and EU Seller Central pages for automated settings, auto removal date, stranded SKUs, and available quantities as they will differ based on the location of your inventory." [official] (News_Amazon, "FBA: New automated removal settings to fix stranded inventory in EU and UK", https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/2b6a7b3a1280e8149945f6a9d2d80227, posted 2021-05-06, read 2026-11-10).

A9. **The high-price stranding.** "Automate Pricing … sets minimum and maximum price thresholds that apply throughout Seller Central - even when you're not actively repricing." "Amazon has automatic validation that limits maximum prices to less than 6 times the ASIN's recent sales price or $200, whichever is higher." A third cause is detection under the Marketplace Fair Pricing Policy. [official, forum staff] (Manny_Amazon, reply in "Why a high price error, when I am using the lowest price?", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/86f2db02-74e7-479c-8a72-cab293f5e1cf, thread opened 2025-10-11, read 2026-11-10). This is a moderator's account, US store; the listing playbook owns the price rules.

A10. **Other facts from the help page** [official, read 2026-10-14 by the listing playbook's seat] (*Fix stranded inventory*, G201436600, version 19; the page carries the banner "The content in this help page will no longer be updated"):
- "If you have units of an ASIN stranded …, do not send additional inventory of that ASIN … your shipment will not be processed."
- "If you address the cause for the stranded inventory yourself, allow 48 hours for the updates to reflect in your stranded inventory."
- Amazon creates two SKU formats itself: `Amazon.Found.Bxxxxxxxxx`, when "your product is not labeled correctly", and `FBAXXXXXXX.Missing#`, created "during an inbound shipment investigation". Units under them appear stranded because "the system-generated SKU does not correspond to an active listing". With no listing created they "will be removed from our fulfillment centers based on your auto-removal settings".

A11. **The stranded report.** `GET_STRANDED_INVENTORY_UI_DATA`, "FBA Stranded Inventory Report": "Contains a breakdown of inventory in stranded status, including recommended actions. Content updated in near real-time." Roles: Pricing, Amazon Fulfillment. Request only. Documented columns, in order: `primary-action`, `date-stranded`, `Date-to-take-auto-removal`, `status-primary`, `status-secondary`, `error-message`, `stranded-reason`, `asin`, `sku`, `fnsku`, `product-name`, `condition`, `fulfilled-by`, `fulfillable-qty`, `your-price`, `unfulfillable-qty`, `reserved-quantity`, `inbound-shipped-qty`. [official] (Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) Reports, https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/report-type-values-fba, page stamped 2026-10-13, read 2026-11-10). **The documented list has no `date-classified-as-unsellable` column**; it has `Date-to-take-auto-removal`. [seat] That matches A6: since 2020-06-30 aged stranded stock is removed rather than reclassified, so the older column would have no meaning. Whether a live file still carries the old header is open (O2). The page does not define the values of `primary-action`, `status-primary`, `status-secondary` or `stranded-reason`.

A12. **The bulk-fix report has lost its feed.** `GET_STRANDED_INVENTORY_LOADER_DATA`: "Contains a list of stranded inventory. Update the listing information in this report file and then submit the file using the Flat File Inventory Loader Feed (`POST_FLAT_FILE_INVLOADER_DATA`) of the Feeds API." Roles: Pricing, Amazon Fulfillment. [official] (same page). But `POST_FLAT_FILE_INVLOADER_DATA` is on the list of "Feed types that will be sunset on March 31, 2025" [official] (Deprecation of Feeds API support for XML and Flat File Listings Feeds, https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/changelog/deprecation-of-feeds-api-support-for-xml-and-flat-file-listings-feeds, read 2026-11-10), and the Feed Type Values page (stamped 2026-11-05) lists only `JSON_LISTINGS_FEED` under Listings [official] (https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/feed-type-values, read 2026-11-10). The deprecation notice also says: "This deprecation does not impact any listings feeds submitted by sellers directly through Seller Central." So the report still exists, its documented submit path through the API does not, and the reports page has not been corrected. The report's columns (`sku`, `product-id`, `price`, `item-condition`, `quantity`, `add-delete`, …) remain a readable list of stranded SKUs.

A13. **Stranded stock and cost.** The IPI has four parts: "excess inventory, 90-day sell-through, stranded inventory and in-stock rate" [official, read 2026-10-14 by the listing playbook's seat] (*Inventory performance*, G202174810, version 9; the four parts are that seat's summary, not a quote). No Amazon sentence opened in this session says in terms that stranded units keep accruing storage fees or count toward capacity and age. Amazon's fee wording is general: monthly storage "apply to all units held in Amazon fulfillment centers" [official, forum staff] (Christine_Amazon, A-C5 below), and a practitioner says it in terms (P1). An exemption for stranded units was found nowhere.

### B. Excess inventory

B1. **Definition.** "We consider an item to be excess if it has over 90 days of supply based on the forecasted demand." [official, read 2026-10-14 by the listing playbook's seat] (*Inventory performance*, G202174810, version 9). An Amazon forum staffer repeated the sentence [snippet]. A moderator's checklist uses the same figure: "ASINs with 90+ days of supply on hand" [official, forum staff] (Christine_Amazon, "FBA Storage 2026: Prepare Now & Save Money", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/aec44886-cf06-4fbc-a42b-e7c8dfa9a225, posted 2026-03-10, read 2026-11-10). The basis is Amazon's forecast, which no read exposes; the 90 is Amazon's definition, not a threshold of ours.

B2. **Amazon's own stocking guidance sits below the excess line.** "A good guideline is to maintain enough inventory to cover 30 to 60 days of your expected sales." [official, read 2026-10-14 by the listing playbook's seat] (same page and the IPI FAQ, GZJF4DY2W6MERBAL, version 19). A moderator wrote "Avoid sending in more inventory than you can sell in 60–90 days." [official, forum staff] (Christine_Amazon, same post as B1). The two guidelines differ; both are guidance.

B3. **The planning report's excess and recommendation columns.** `GET_FBA_INVENTORY_PLANNING_DATA`, "FBA Manage Inventory Health Report": "Provides a consolidated view across your sales (7, 30, 60, and 90 days), shipment statuses (working, received, and shipped), estimated storage and long-term storage fees, and aged and excess units. It includes metrics across sales, inbound, estimated fees, and recommended actions. Not available in the NL, PL, SE, or BE stores." "Attributes vary by store. The Brazil (BR) store returns a reduced set of attributes, but includes unique long-term-storage and restock values." Role: **Amazon Fulfillment only**. Request only. [official] (Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) Reports, read 2026-11-10). Columns that bear on excess: `estimated-excess-quantity`, `days-of-supply`, `weeks-of-cover-t30`, `weeks-of-cover-t90`, `sell-through`, `alert`, `recommended-action`, `recommended-sales-price`, `recommended-sale-duration-days`, `recommended-removal-quantity`, `estimated-cost-savings-of-recommended-actions`, `no-sale-last-6-months`, `your-price`, `sales-price`, `lowest-price-new-plus-shipping`, `lowest-price-used`, `featuredoffer-price`. The page lists **`DEPRECATED healthy-inventory-level`** in those words. The page defines no column and lists no value set for `recommended-action` or `alert`. Brazil only: `weeks-of-cover`, `weeks-of-cover-estimated-excess-quantity`, `recommended-order-quantity`, `recommended-order-date`. (Gap fill 2026-11-10, same page re-read by the verifier) the list also carries `is-seasonal-in-next-3-months`, `season-name`, `season-start-date` and `season-end-date`, `fba-inventory-level-health-status`, `Inventory Supply at FBA`, `Total Reserved Quantity`, `Total Days of Supply (including units from open shipments)` and `pending-removal-quantity`; the page defines none of them. Unlike the stranded, returns and removal reports, the planning report's entry states no update frequency ("near real-time" or "daily").

B4. **Where Amazon shows excess in Seller Central.** "We've launched a new FBA Inventory Age & Excess Analytics page … Provides a summarized view of how your aging inventory levels are trending over time; Shows you how other metrics like FBA sales and aged inventory surcharge match up with your aging inventory; Summarizes excess inventory recommendations and aged inventory surcharges. The page contains one year of historical data." [official] (News_Amazon, "Analyze aging and excess FBA inventory with new analytics page", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/6e333f37-0988-43ef-98ad-6a1ff6b084cc, posted 2024-05-27, read 2026-11-10). Amazon staff still name "Manage Excess Inventory" as a page a liquidation order can be created from (Bryce_Amazon, 2024-08-22, E-L1) and as a resource (Christine_Amazon, 2026-03-10). Outlet recommendations are "in the FBA Inventory tool" (E-O1).

B5. **Retired.** `GET_EXCESS_INVENTORY_DATA`, `GET_FBA_FULFILLMENT_INVENTORY_HEALTH_DATA` and `GET_FBA_INVENTORY_AGED_DATA`: deprecation date March 31, 2022; removal date June 27, 2022. [official] (SP-API Deprecation Schedule, https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/sp-api-deprecations, read 2026-11-10). The planning report (B3) is their one successor.

B6. **Excess can block inbound.** A forum thread quotes Amazon blocking an AWD-to-FBA transfer with "You already have high days of supply for this FNSKU in our fulfillment centers" [snippet]. Owned by `areas/inbound.md` and `areas/restock-and-forecast.md`.

### C. Aged inventory

C1. **The age columns of the planning report** [official] (Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) Reports, read 2026-11-10):
- Coarse: `inv-age-0-to-90-days`, `inv-age-91-to-180-days`, `inv-age-181-to-270-days`, `inv-age-271-to-365-days`.
- Finer: `inv-age-0-to-30-days`, `inv-age-31-to-60-days`, `inv-age-61-to-90-days`, `inv-age-181-to-330-days`, `inv-age-331-to-365-days`.
- Over a year: `inv-age-365-plus-days` "(not available in the US, DE, UK, FR, IT, and ES stores)"; in those six stores instead `inv-age-366-to-455-days` and `inv-age-456-plus-days` ("Available for US, DE, UK, FR, IT, and Amazon ES stores").
- Surcharge exposure, by band: `quantity-to-be-charged-ais-<band>` and `estimated-ais-<band>` for 181-210, 211-240, 241-270, 271-300, 301-330, 331-365, then 365-plus (not in the six stores) or 366-455 and 456-plus (the six stores).
- `Inventory age snapshot date`, `snapshot-date`, `estimated-storage-cost-next-month`, `units-shipped-t7`, `-t30`, `-t60`, `-t90`, `sales-shipped-last-7-days` … `-90-days`, `sell-through`, `storage-type`, `storage-volume`, `item-volume`, `pending-removal-quantity`, `unfulfillable-quantity`.
- Brazil only: `qty-to-be-charged-ltsf-6-mo`, `projected-ltsf-6-mo`, `qty-to-be-charged-ltsf-12-mo`, `estimated-ltsf-next-charge`.

The bands in the file are fixed column names; the two 181-and-over coarse sets overlap (181-270 / 271-365 and 181-330 / 331-365), so they are two cuts of the same units, not addable.

C2. **The monthly cut-off (US, as a moderator states it).** Surcharges "are assessed monthly on the 15th of each month and charged between the 18th-22nd". "Submit a removal or disposal order before the cleanup date (deadline: 11:59 PM PT on the 14th of each month). Once submitted, you won't incur aged inventory surcharges on those units, even if they aren't physically removed before the cleanup date." "Use the FBA Inventory tool to identify which ASINs are currently subject to or will be subject to the aged inventory surcharge within 60 days." [official, forum staff] (Angie_Amazon, "AVOID Costly Fees: Aging Inventory Action Plan", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/85f60e98-f93c-48fe-bee7-07917616f827, posted 2026-02-09, read 2026-11-10). The same post gives the US surcharge schedule "Effective January 16, 2026" in eight bands from 181-210 days to 456+ days, with the three bands from 181 to 270 days excluding "clothing, shoes, bags, jewelry, and watches"; the amounts are `areas/holding-costs.md`'s, and the charged amounts are read from `GET_FBA_FULFILLMENT_LONGTERM_STORAGE_FEE_CHARGES_DATA` (R6).

C3. **The 2027 change.** "Effective February 1, 2027, we'll eliminate the storage utilization surcharge … We'll also remove the aged inventory surcharge and combine it with monthly storage fees into a single, age-based rate. These changes will result in flat or lower fees for inventory that turns in under six months, higher fees for aged inventory". [official] (Update to US Referral and Fulfillment by Amazon fees for 2027, https://sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com/update-to-us-referral-and-fulfillment-by-amazon-fees-for-2027, published 2026-11-05, read 2026-11-10). US. The announcement, as opened, says nothing about removal, disposal or liquidation fees. [seat] After 2027-02-01 the `quantity-to-be-charged-ais-*` and `estimated-ais-*` columns describe a fee that no longer exists in the US; what the report will carry instead is open (O6).

C4. **Automated removal of aged sellable stock ("Automated fulfillable settings").** "Once you've enabled automated removals, we'll return, liquidate, or dispose of the following: Fulfillable inventory that has been in a fulfillment center for more than 365 days; Units of ASINs that have not sold for six or more consecutive months and that have been in a fulfillment center for more than 180 days. For sellers of highly-seasonal products, that second bullet is very important to consider before enabling automatic removal as your ASIN will not be able to be shipped back into FBA for a waiting period of up to 90 days after its removal." [official, forum staff] (Topher_Amazon, "FBA Automatic Removal Settings", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f2c4e9b1-c146-4a12-80bf-e721429ac36e, posted 2024-03-07, read 2026-11-10). Angie_Amazon repeats the two triggers (2026-02-09) and adds "Once a removal order is created, storage fees stop during the processing period until completion." Amazon's texts disagree on the default:
- Topher_Amazon (2024-03-07) and Angie_Amazon (2026-02-09) describe it as something the seller enables.
- A forum search summary quotes Amazon: "you're opted in to the automated fulfillable inventory removal services by default" [snippet].
- "If you haven't opted into automated removals and your inventory becomes long-term storage, Amazon will automatically dispose of units 30 days after they become long-term storage. This is a forced policy to keep fulfillment centers efficient." [official, forum staff] (Angie_Amazon, "FBA Removal Orders: What Triggers Destruction and How to Avoid It", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/d16c5413-df01-430b-b5f8-f270bbbc9057, posted 2026-04-01, read 2026-11-10). "Long-term storage" is not defined in the post.
- From 2025-09-30 (US, Canada), a seller who has configured neither the automated fulfillable nor the automated unfulfillable settings is "automatically … enrolled in the Liquidations program" (E-L3).

### D. Unsellable (unfulfillable) stock

D1. **The six dispositions.** `getInventorySummaries` returns, under `inventoryDetails.unfulfillableQuantity`: `totalUnfulfillableQuantity`, `customerDamagedQuantity` ("units in customer damaged disposition"), `warehouseDamagedQuantity`, `distributorDamagedQuantity`, `carrierDamagedQuantity`, `defectiveQuantity`, `expiredQuantity`. [official] (local model `models/fba-inventory-api-model/fbaInventory.json:750-782`). The same object's other members are `fulfillableQuantity`, the three inbound counts, `reservedQuantity` and `researchingQuantity`; **there is no stranded field** (`fbaInventory.json:784` on). Role: Amazon Fulfillment or Product Listing (Role Mappings for SP-API Operations).

D2. **What "unfulfillable" means, in Amazon's words.** "When inventory that is in a fulfillment center is not in sellable condition, its status changes to unfulfillable and it is set aside and must be removed from the FBA network." [official, forum staff] (Topher_Amazon, 2024-03-07, same post as C4).

D3. **"Customer damaged" does not mean broken.** "The 'Customer Damaged' label doesn't necessarily mean the item itself was physically broken. Per Amazon's own return condition definitions, it means the unit was returned in a condition that cannot be resold as new and yes, this can include simply opening the packaging." "Under standard FBA policy, Amazon does not automatically reimburse sellers for Customer Damaged returns, as the fault is attributed to the customer rather than Amazon." "When a return is graded 'Customer Damaged,' the item stays in your unfulfillable inventory … You can request a removal order to have it sent back to you, inspect it, and potentially relist it as used or refurbished." [official, forum staff] (Aria_Amazon, reply in "'Customer damaged' returns", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/c578f00f-6fa8-45d0-a798-6be008b0e43e, thread opened 2026-02-19, read 2026-11-10).

D4. **Where the dispositions come from: the returns report.** `GET_FBA_FULFILLMENT_CUSTOMER_RETURNS_DATA`, "FBA Returns Report": "Contains customer returned items received at an Amazon fulfillment center, including Return Reason and Disposition. Content updated daily." Roles: Pricing, Amazon Fulfillment. Columns: `return-date`, `order-id`, `sku`, `asin`, `fnsku`, `product-name`, `quantity`, `fulfillment-center-id`, `detailed-disposition`, `reason`, `status`, `license-plate-number`, `customer-comments`. [official] (Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) Reports, read 2026-11-10). The page lists no values for `detailed-disposition`, `reason` or `status`. Two `status` values are named by Amazon staff: "Pending Repackaging — Your unit is currently being repackaged" and "Repackaged Successfully — Your unit has been restored to sellable inventory" (D5). Third-party lists of disposition values (SELLABLE, DAMAGED, CUSTOMER_DAMAGED, CARRIER_DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, EXPIRED) were seen only in search summaries [snippet]. Warehouse, carrier and distributor damage and expiry do not come from customer returns; the ledger carries them (R8).

D5. **Repackaging is automatic; refurbishment is a setting.** "If the packaging is damaged but the product itself is in good condition, FBA will automatically repackage eligible items so they can be resold as new. … This service is automatic for eligible items and can't be disabled". Optional refurbishment adds "Re-taping, re-gluing, and re-stapling boxes", label removal, re-boxing, and "For shoes and apparel: steaming and removing stains and odors". The post lists the categories repackaging covers and names "Returns Evaluation Settings", where a seller can opt "specific ASINs in or out of the standard evaluation process". [official, forum staff] (Indy_Amazon, "FBA Repackaging After Returns: Avoid Unsellable Status", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/742360f6-f181-4f9b-90f0-120e58a11773, posted 2026-07-11, read 2026-11-10). Since 2021-09-09 refurbishment is also an automated-removal option: "Eligible customer-returned products that are in new condition but require low-touch refurbishing will be returned to sellable status at no cost to you." [official] (News_Amazon, "Refurbishment and liquidation are now automated-removal options", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/aad6cb0ba00e0171101dd773bf4b9057, posted 2021-09-09, read 2026-11-10).

### E. The ways out

#### E-R. Removal (return to an address) and disposal

E-R1. **What an order can be.** "Return to Seller: Items are shipped back to your designated address; Liquidation: Sell items to professional liquidators through Amazon; Bulk Removal: Remove multiple SKUs simultaneously", and disposal orders: "Items are permanently removed from inventory". [official, forum staff] (TaylorR_Amazon, "Master Your FBA Inventory: Strategic Guide to Removals & Disposals", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/fa5e35d6-091d-4712-8b6c-63a15535d5bd, posted 2025-12-05, read 2026-11-10).

E-R2. **Processing time: Amazon gives five different figures.**
- "typically 5-14 days" (TaylorR_Amazon, 2025-12-05).
- "Removal orders may take up to 14 business days to process" (Angie_Amazon, 2026-02-09).
- "While there is no exact timeline for removal orders, they typically take 10 to 14 business days to pick, pack, and process. However, the time may increase to 90 days or more during peak removal periods. After the shipment has left the fulfillment center, please allow an additional two weeks for the carrier to deliver." [official, forum staff] (Jameson_Amazon, reply in https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/8549f08b-ed2c-45f1-a634-671f7c96e2cf, thread opened 2025-09-18, read 2026-11-10).
- Japan: "Currently, removal order processing may take up to 30 days or more due to the peak removal periods." [official, forum staff] (Forum_moderator_3, "FAQ about Fulfillment Center operation (removal order)", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f960626b-6403-42a4-aaaf-49babd81a559, last update 2025-08-12, read 2026-11-10).

[official] (News_Amazon, https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/40c3e20dea9848d668e2ef13c846c261, posted 2021-10-12, read 2026-11-10). No 2026 holiday notice was found.

Amazon's 2026 fee announcement promised to "provide faster removals processing" [official] (News_Amazon, "2026 Updates to US Referral and Fulfillment by Amazon Fees", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f3fa3211-820b-4e2e-a023-158a9cf55f99, read 2026-11-10). E-R3. **Cancelling.** "You can cancel the order if it's in Planning status. Removal orders usually are in Planning status for only a few hours. If the status is Processing, you will not be able to cancel the order. During peak periods such as Prime Day, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and the holidays, removal orders can stay in Processing status for several weeks." [official, forum staff] (Dominic_Amazon, reply in "Cancel Removal Order", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/49449602-838e-4e8b-8b56-bb4931b9149e, thread opened 2024-10-23, read 2026-11-10). Statuses Amazon staff name: Planning, Processing (Dominic_Amazon); "Pending" until the inventory ships, then "Completed" (Bryce_Amazon, for liquidation orders); "Pending" also in the capacity rule (E-R5). Orders Amazon creates cannot be cancelled: a required removal (D8), a UK/EU disposal after notice (D7), a UK automated removal (C4). A liquidation order cannot be cancelled once confirmed (E-L4).

E-R4. **When the fee is charged.** US: "Effective March 1, Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) removal and disposal fees will be charged on a per-unit basis at the time each unit is removed or disposed of. The fee rates for removal and disposal fees remain unchanged. … This change will apply automatically to new orders created on or after March 1." The date was revised from February 15, 2026. "To review your shipment level charges and when units are removed, go to Payments and click Transaction view." [official] (News_Amazon, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/2f4edad8-31d4-443b-8b22-12776c4f685a, posted 2026-01-29, read 2026-11-10). E-R5. **What a removal order stops, and when.**

#### E-L. FBA Liquidations

E-L1. **How it works.** "Amazon will work with contracted liquidators to sell your excess or customer-returned inventory in bulk. The liquidators will purchase this inventory for a 'gross recovery value' which is typically 5-10% of the product's original value. Amazon will then deduct processing and referral fees, and pay you the 'net recovery value' - which should hit your account within 90 days of submitting the order." "Amazon charges two fees …: A per-item processing fee based on the item's size and weight; A 15% referral fee on the gross recovery value. For example, a 0.2 lb wireless phone case with an average selling price of $20 could have a net recovery value of around $1 after fees." "You can create a liquidation order directly from various inventory management pages in Seller Central, such as Manage Excess Inventory or the Recommended Removal report. Once you submit the order, your monthly storage fees and aged inventory surcharges will stop accruing. The inventory will then ship to a liquidator within 30 days, and you should see the net recovery value in your payments within 60-90 days." ### F. What the API shows of stuck stock

F1. `getInventorySummaries` has no stranded count; stranded units are not separated from fulfillable or unfulfillable ones in its fields (D1). The stranded report (A11) is the read.

F2. The MYI reports (`GET_FBA_MYI_UNSUPPRESSED_INVENTORY_DATA`, "active (not archived)"; `GET_FBA_MYI_ALL_INVENTORY_DATA`, "all (including archived)"; roles Pricing, Amazon Fulfillment) carry `afn-listing-exists`, `mfn-listing-exists`, `afn-fulfillable-quantity`, `afn-unsellable-quantity`, `afn-researching-quantity` and no stranded column [official] (Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) Reports). [seat] A row with units and no FBA listing is the nearest signal; "Unsuppressed" in the type name suggests suppressed listings are left out of that one, which the page does not state.

F3. The Listings Items API reports why an offer is not buyable: `status` values `BUYABLE` and `DISCOVERABLE`, and per issue an `enforcements.actions[].action` of `LISTING_SUPPRESSED` ("takes down the current listing item's buyability"), `ATTRIBUTE_SUPPRESSED`, `CATALOG_ITEM_REMOVED` ("This catalog item is inactive on Amazon, and all offers against it … are non-buyable") or `SEARCH_SUPPRESSED` ("hidden from search results"), with an `exemption` of `EXEMPT`, `EXEMPT_UNTIL_EXPIRY_DATE` (and `expiryDate`) or `NOT_EXEMPT`. [official] (local model `models/listings-items-api-model/listingsItems_2021-08-01.json:1847`, `:3707`). `getListingsItem`, `searchListingsItems`, `patchListingsItem`: role Product Listing (or Inventory and Order Tracking). [seat] `SEARCH_SUPPRESSED` leaves the offer buyable, so on Amazon's definition (A1) it does not strand stock by itself; `EXEMPT_UNTIL_EXPIRY_DATE` is the nearest thing in the API to an "at risk" state.

F4. `GET_MERCHANTS_LISTINGS_FYP_REPORT`, "Suppressed Listings Report": "Contains suppressed listings, the reason each listing is suppressed, and instructions for removing each suppression." Role: Product Listing. Requested or scheduled. One marketplace per report. Columns: `Status`, `Reason`, `SKU`, `ASIN`, `Product name`, `Condition`, `Status Change Date`, `Issue Description`. It carries a UTF-8 byte order mark, and "Reports are not cached by locale". [official] (Inventory Reports, https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/report-type-values-inventory, read 2026-11-10). It has no quantity column.

F5. The inventory ledger: `GET_LEDGER_SUMMARY_VIEW_DATA` (by `Disposition`: `Customer Returns`, `Vendor Returns`, `Found`, `Lost`, `Damaged`, `Disposed`, `Other Events`, `Unknown Events`, start and end balance) and `GET_LEDGER_DETAIL_VIEW_DATA` ("products that are sold, returned, removed/disposed, damaged, lost, and found … for 18 months"; `eventType` filter `Adjustments`, `CustomerReturns`, `Receipts`, `Shipments`, `VendorReturns`, `WhseTransfers`; columns `Event Type`, `Reference ID`, `Quantity`, `Fulfillment Center`, `Disposition`, `Reason`, `Reconciled Quantity`, `Unreconciled Quantity`). Role: Amazon Fulfillment only. [official] (Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) Reports). The page does not list the disposition or reason codes.

F6. Report freshness: "A near real-time FBA report is generated no more than once every 30 minutes. … A daily FBA report is generated no more than once every four hours." [official] (same page).

F7. Notifications: `FBA_INVENTORY_AVAILABILITY_CHANGES` (role Amazon Fulfillment) "is sent whenever there is a change in the Fulfillment By Amazon (FBA) inventory quantities" and carries fulfillable and `Unfulfillable` counts per store; `LISTINGS_ITEM_STATUS_CHANGE` and `LISTINGS_ITEM_ISSUES_CHANGE` (role Product Listing) carry the listing side. [official] (Notification Type Values, https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/notification-type-values; Role Mappings for SP-API Types; read 2026-11-10). F8. Data Kiosk: the release notes of September 30, 2026 say the Analytics Economics dataset was "Updated … with deal, coupon, removal, disposal, and fuel fees" [official] (SP-API Release Notes). ### P1. Stranded stock: causes, defaults and habits

- **Jungle Scout** (Brian Connolly, "8 Reasons for Amazon Stranded Inventory and How to Address Them", https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-stranded-inventory/, dated 2023-08-13) heads eight causes: "Pricing error," "Restricted ASIN," "Suspended listing," "Listing error," "Brand qualification required," "Expired product," "Closing or deleting a listing," and "Bulk upload template error." He says that with no action "within 30 days of the date it became stranded" the stock is removed, that "Amazon has 'Dispose of units' set as the default setting for all of these stranded inventory scenarios", and "If you want your inventory spared, choose 'Return units to me' as your removal method." The article also says Amazon keeps charging monthly storage on stranded units and that stranded stock lowers the IPI. [opinion]
- **My Amazon Guy** (Steven Pope, "How to Fix Amazon Stranded Inventory Quickly and Easily to Recover Lost Sales", https://myamazonguy.com/?p=18120, updated 2026-03-27): "If no action is taken, Amazon will automatically remove or dispose of stranded inventory after 30 days." "Check your stranded inventory dashboard at least once a week." He sets stranded items to "Return units to you" and enables automatic relisting "for common reasons like 'Listing Closed' or 'Merchant Fulfilled'". [opinion]
- **EcomCrew** (Christine Gerzon, "What Is Amazon Stranded Inventory and How Do You Fix It?", https://www.ecomcrew.com/amazon-stranded-inventory-and-how-do-you-fix-it/, updated 2023-12-05): "The worst part is the default action is to have that inventory disposed of." She treats the 30-day rule as the older one and says removal follows the seller's settings since 2020-06-30, which agrees with A6. [opinion]
- **领星ERP** (Lingxing, "什么是亚马逊滞留库存？ 如何解决亚马逊滞留库存问题？", https://www.lingxing.com/article/1852.html, dated 2023-03-31), on Amazon Australia from 2023-03-30: "默认情况下，亚马逊库存滞留90天后将进行移除，除非您已在自己的设置中设定好了周期。" Gloss: by default stranded stock is removed after 90 days unless the seller set another period; with no valid Australian return address it is disposed of. [opinion; Lingxing's account of Amazon]
- A **知无不言** seller (post of 2021-08-23, https://www.wearesellers.com/question/43107; replies behind login, question only): several listings were merged by Amazon, the seller closed all but one, and the closed listings' stock was disposed of automatically: "从生成移除订单到弃置完成，整个过程在24小时以内。" "他们说自动弃置的订单无法取消". Gloss: from the order being generated to disposal complete took under 24 hours; support said an automatic disposal order cannot be cancelled. The seller had the longest auto-removal period set and says the stock had been unsellable under 30 days. [opinion; one seller's report]
- **白鲸出海** (poster "yemeifen", crediting 三头六臂跨境电商联盟, https://www.baijing.cn/article/41009, dated 2022-10-11; through the fetch reader): "自 2022 年 10 月 14 日起，亚马逊将自动移除在运营中心存放超过 14 天的不可售库存。" "在此之前，移除操作适用于在运营中心存放超过 30 天的不可售库存。" Gloss: from 2022-10-14 Amazon removes unsellable stock held over 14 days, down from 30. Their warning: a listing taken down for infringement or as a restricted product leaves much less time to appeal before the stock goes. [opinion; their account of Amazon]

The day counts practitioners give for the same event differ by source, store and year: 30 days (Jungle Scout 2023, My Amazon Guy 2026), seller-configured (EcomCrew), 90 days in Australia (Lingxing 2023), 60 days for unsellable stock in Europe (易仓 2026, P4), 14 days for unsellable stock (白鲸出海 2022). That spread matches A7 and D6-D8: the number is not stable.

### P2. When to act: age and cover triggers (theirs)

- **My Amazon Guy** (Ken Zhou, "Avoid Stockouts and Overstocks", https://myamazonguy.com/articles/selling-on-amazon/amazon-stockouts-and-overstock/, updated 2026-09-04): "Under 35 days is your stockout watchlist, and over 120 days is your overstock watchlist." "Start moving slow stock at day 90, not day 180." Its KPI table targets under 10% of inventory aged over 120 days and three or more turns a quarter. [opinion]
- **Titan Network** (Dan Ashburn, "Aged Inventory Guide", https://titannetwork.com/aged-inventory-guide-cut-fba-fees-boost-margins/, dated 2026-02-03): "Weeks of cover above 12 signals overstock risk." "Above 20? You're likely to hit 181 days before selling through." For stock aged 91-180 days "drop price 15-25% via a Lightning Deal or coupon"; removal "for any SKU past 270 days with under 2 units sold weekly"; "Compare removal fees against six months of projected surcharges to calculate the break-even point." [opinion]
- **Seller Labs** ("Aged Inventory Surcharges", https://www.sellerlabs.com/knowledge-base/aged-inventory-surcharges-clearing-stock-before-it-costs-you/, updated 2026-08-24): "the 121–180 day range is where cheap action is still possible." [opinion]
- **Nova** (novadata.io, https://novadata.io/resources/answers/removal-vs-disposal-when-to-choose, dated 2026-07-26): "Trigger the decision at 181 days when the aged surcharge starts, not at 365 days when the fee has compounded for 6 months." [opinion]
- **Webgility** (Yash Bodane, https://www.webgility.com/blog/amazon-excess-inventory, dated 2026-01-29): "Focus on SKUs with 90 or more days of supply"; its high class is "more than 180 days of supply based on trailing sales". [opinion]
- **Aura** (Colleen Quattlebaum, https://goaura.com/blog/liquidation-strategies-for-amazon-resellers, dated 2025-09-18), as an example rule: "liquidate any SKU that hasn't sold 5 units in 90 days." [opinion]
- **Jungle Scout** (Brian Connolly, "Amazon Inventory Management 2024 Guide", https://www.junglescout.com/resources/articles/amazon-inventory-management/, dated 2023-12-14): "A sell-through rate is over 7 is considered excellent." "If your sell-through rate is less than 1, this means you held more inventory than you sold in the past 90 days." [opinion]
- **eComEngine** (Bob Norberg, "Dead Inventory Detox", https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/amazon-dead-inventory-guide, updated 2026-06-24) gives tests without numbers: "Hold only when you can clearly explain what will cause the SKU to recover" "No explanation? No hold." "If I had zero units of this today, would I buy it again?" And on discounting: "A discount can move inventory. It can also train the market to ignore you until you panic again." [opinion]
- **赛狐ERP** (SellFox, "亚马逊库存滞销怎么处理？避坑要点与工具实操攻略", https://www.sellfox.com/blog/article/ymx-kczx-sellfox, page date 2026-08-07): "库龄超过90天，就要开始持续观察，超过150天，必须落地对应的处理方案，要是临近 181 天还毫无动作，基本就是等着被扣超龄库存附加费。等到库龄走到271天之后，费用会出现明显跳涨，一旦库存超过365天，务必要果断止损。" Gloss: past 90 days of age start watching; past 150 a plan must be in place; doing nothing near day 181 means waiting for the surcharge; after day 271 the fee jumps; past 365 cut the loss. Its three tiers: light (90-180 days, some sales: reprice, coupon, Prime discount, Outlet); medium (180-365 days: off-Amazon discounts, bundles, MCF, other marketplaces); heavy (over 365 days: FBA Liquidations, removal, disposal, return to an overseas warehouse). "能卖就抓紧卖，卖不动就尽快移，没有残值果断断舍离。" (Sell what can sell, move out what cannot, drop what has no residual value.) On restock: "当可售周数超过12周，反问自己一句这批货能不能在181天以内消化完毕" (above 12 weeks of cover, ask whether it sells through within 181 days). [opinion]

## Published research

Read by a delegated reader on 2026-11-09; its notes, with what was read in full and what only as an abstract, are `scratch/stock-playbook/stuck-stock/research-notes.md`. `beyond/sellers-tools-and-research.md` owns the wider survey. Stated plainly: **no published study was found on Amazon sellers' excess or aged stock, on how FBA storage fees change seller behaviour, on recovery rates from FBA liquidation, removal or donation, or on the effect of a sole seller's price cut on Amazon sales rank.** What exists is store retail and theory.

PR1. **Purchase cost is sunk; the price of stock already owned depends on units left, time left and the salvage floor.** Gallego and van Ryzin (1994), "Optimal Dynamic Pricing of Inventories with Stochastic Demand over Finite Horizons", *Management Science* 40(8), 999-1020 (full PDF read, https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/3943/vanryzin_optimal_dynamic_pricing.pdf): "all costs related to the purchase or production of items are considered sunk costs"; "at a given point in time, the optimal price drops as the inventory increases; conversely, for a given level of inventory, the optimal price rises if we have more time to sell." A salvage value is handled as the floor the price works above. Limits: theory; a monopoly seller with a known demand function, no competitors, no ranking feedback. This is an assumption of the model, not a measurement. [research]

PR2. **The salvage value sets the cost of a leftover unit.** Schweitzer and Cachon (2000), *Management Science* 46(3), 404-420 (full PDF read, https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2012/04/Cachon_schweitzer_ms.pdf): the profit-maximising order quantity q satisfies F(q) = (p − c)/(p − s); "The ratio (p − c)/(p − s) is called the critical fractile". Overage cost is c − s. The form with a holding cost added was on no page opened. It governs how much to buy, not what to do with stock already bought. Cachon and Kök (2007), on how to estimate a salvage value when the clearance price is chosen after demand is seen, could not be opened (HTTP 403 on every copy); its claim that average past clearance recovery overstates what the next surplus unit fetches is [snippet]. [research]

PR3. **Measured gain from disciplined markdowns: about 4 to 6% of clearance revenue, all store apparel, 1993-2008.** Caro and Gallien (2012), *Operations Research* 60(6) (working paper of 2011-06-10 read in full, https://escholarship.org/content/qt0fm8d8sv/qt0fm8d8sv_noSplash_6ab96251f7597df9f88dae7728229121.pdf): "As part of a controlled field experiment conducted in all Belgian and Irish stores during the 2008 Fall-Winter season, this new process increased clearance revenues by approximately 6%." The model "usually made price suggestions that were slightly more aggressive at the beginning and more conservative towards the end." Heching, Gallego and van Ryzin (2002) estimate about 4% at one apparel retailer on Spring 1993 data, from a fitted model replayed on history (abstract only). Smith and Achabal (1998) report 4% year on year at the best of three chains, with no controls (abstract, and second-hand through Caro and Gallien). Only the Zara study is a controlled experiment. None concerns an online marketplace. [research]

PR4. **The measured managerial error is late, deep markdowns aimed at emptying the shelf, not holding too long.** Caro and Sáez de Tejada Cuenca (2023), *Manufacturing & Service Operations Management* 25(2) (accepted manuscript, abstract and introduction read, https://escholarship.org/content/qt0vv430r5/qt0vv430r5.pdf?t=rrksmy): "managers ignored the DSS's recommendations too often", because "inventory and sales were more salient to managers than revenue"; "an incremental week to deplete the inventory was associated with a 2.3% increase in the probability of deviating". One firm, professional pricing managers. No published study measuring sunk-cost bias in markdown or liquidation timing was found; Arkes and Blumer (1985) on the sunk-cost effect in general was not opened [snippet]. The practitioners' common claim that sellers hold too long (P2) has no direct research support in what was read. [research]

PR5. **Why starting high can be rational.** Lazear (1986), *American Economic Review* 76(1) (NBER abstract only, https://www.nber.org/papers/w1446): "It is sometimes better to set a high price and to risk no sale". Bitran and Mondschein (1997) (abstract only): changing price only at set review dates costs little ("the loss experienced when implementing periodic pricing reviews instead of continuous policies is small"). [research]

PR6. **Delay has a measured price in two product classes.** Guide, Souza, Van Wassenhove and Blackburn (2006), "Time Value of Commercial Product Returns", *Management Science* 52(8) (author manuscript read, https://cdn.vanderbilt.edu/vu-web/owen/files/publications/Time_Value_of_Commercial_ReturnsManagementScience.pdf): decay of "1% per week" for Hewlett-Packard inkjet printers and "1% per month" for Bosch power tools; for "short life cycle, time-sensitive products ... these losses can exceed 30% of product value." Two manufacturers, data of about 1999-2004, customer returns in a manufacturer's refurbishing channel, not unsold overstock at a liquidator. It bears on Grade and Resell and on any return path that takes weeks (E-R2, P4). [research]

PR7. **Recovery rates in liquidation: nothing peer-reviewed found.** Two papers on B2B liquidation auctions (Pilehvar, Elmaghraby and Gopal 2017; Elmaghraby, Gopal and Pilehvar 2018) are about bidding and were not opened [snippet]. Vendor figures such as "20-60% of original cost" are marketing, not research. The only figures are Amazon's own 5-10% (E-L1) and the practitioners' net numbers (P4). [research]

PR8. **On Amazon, adjacent only.** Chen and Tsai (2023), working paper (full PDF, opening sections read, https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2024/program/paper/zF9DnrdS): on listings shared by exactly two sellers, "We estimate an average own-price elasticity of −18.96"; "prices tend to decrease when there are no recent sales". Home & Kitchen on Amazon.com, seven months; it measures which of two sellers wins a shared page, not total demand, and does not apply to a product with one seller. Gleason, Zhang and Wilson (2026), WWW '26 (abstract page read, https://kgi.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Determinants-and-Effects-of-Buy-Box-Suppression-on-Amazon_Gleason-Zhang-Wilson_67.pdf): when Amazon removes the Buy Box, "products immediately fall sharply in search, ad placements completely disappear within 12 hours, and sales ranks are 12% worse after 48 hours." 17,754 products, eight weeks in 2024; it measures suppression, not a price change. [research]

PR9. **Donation and US tax (law, not research; not advice).** 26 U.S.C. § 170(e)(3) (govinfo, 2023 edition, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title26/html/USCODE-2023-title26-subtitleA-chap1-subchapB-partVI-sec170.htm): the enhanced deduction for donated inventory is for a contribution "by a corporation (other than a corporation which is an S corporation)" to a charity that uses it for "the care of the ill, the needy, or infants" and does not sell it; "apparently wholesome food" is open to any business. IRS Publication 526 (2025) (https://www.irs.gov/publications/p526): other donors deduct the lower of market value and basis, and if the stock is worth less than it cost, "your deduction is limited to its FMV." US federal only. Amazon says it "cannot provide tax advice" (E-D1), and no read shows what was donated (S6).

Columns: `stranded-reason`, `primary-action`, `date-stranded`, `Date-to-take-auto-removal`, `status-primary`, `status-secondary`, `error-message`, `fulfillable-qty`, `unfulfillable-qty`, `reserved-quantity`, `inbound-shipped-qty`, `your-price`, `fulfilled-by`. Near real-time; generated at most once every 30 minutes. Pitfalls: value sets are undocumented and localised reason text is likely; the documented header mixes case (`Date-to-take-auto-removal`); a live file may differ from the documented columns (O2); UK and EU stock are separate views (A8); a row here can also be counted as fulfillable elsewhere (O10). `GET_STRANDED_INVENTORY_LOADER_DATA` (PR or AF) lists the same SKUs in loader shape; its submit feed is gone (A12).

R2. **Why the offer is not buyable.** `getListingsItem` / `searchListingsItems` with `includedData=issues,summaries` (Listings Items 2021-08-01). Role: **PL** (or Inventory and Order Tracking). Fields: `summaries[].status`, `issues[].enforcements.actions[].action`, `issues[].enforcements.exemption`, `issues[].severity`. Report `GET_MERCHANTS_LISTINGS_FYP_REPORT`, role **PL**, one marketplace per report, byte order mark, no quantity. Pitfall: the listing side and the stranded report update on different clocks; Amazon says a fix can take 48 hours to show (A10). The listing playbook owns the fix.

R3. **Removal orders.** `GET_FBA_FULFILLMENT_REMOVAL_ORDER_DETAIL_DATA`. Role: **PR or AF**. Columns: `order-id`, `order-source`, `order-type`, `order-status`, `request-date`, `last-updated-date`, `disposition`, `requested-quantity`, `cancelled-quantity`, `disposed-quantity`, `shipped-quantity`, `in-process-quantity`, `removal-fee`, `currency`. Near real-time. Pitfalls: value sets undocumented; orders Amazon created appear beside the seller's (`order-source`); a seller reports liquidations missing (O5); from 2026-03-01 (US) and 2026-05-01 (UK) fees are charged per unit as processed, so `removal-fee` on an open order may be partial (E-R4, [seat]).

R4. **Removal shipments.** `GET_FBA_FULFILLMENT_REMOVAL_SHIPMENT_DETAIL_DATA`. Role: **PR or AF**. Columns: `order-id`, `shipment-date`, `shipped-quantity`, `carrier`, `tracking-number`, `removal-order-type`. Excludes cancelled and disposed items. One order ships in many parcels over weeks (P4).

R5. **Excess, age and Amazon's recommendation.** `GET_FBA_INVENTORY_PLANNING_DATA`. Role: **AF only**. Columns in B3 and C1. The page states no update frequency for this report. Pitfalls: not available in NL, PL, SE, BE; Brazil returns a reduced set; the over-365 columns differ by store; overlapping coarse bands; `healthy-inventory-level` deprecated; `recommended-action` values undocumented; `days-of-supply` and `estimated-excess-quantity` rest on Amazon's forecast; column names mix hyphenated lower case with spaced title case; the `ais` columns describe a US fee ending 2027-02-01 (C3). `areas/restock-and-forecast.md` owns the supply columns.

R6. **What age cost.** `GET_FBA_FULFILLMENT_LONGTERM_STORAGE_FEE_CHARGES_DATA`. Role: **PR or AF**. Columns include `qty-charged`, `amount-charged`, `surcharge-age-tier`, `rate-surcharge`; the requested span "should be equal to one month". Owned by `areas/holding-costs.md`.

R7. **Storage on stock pending removal.** `GET_FBA_STORAGE_FEE_CHARGES_DATA`, column `average_quantity_pending_removal`. Role: **PR or AF**. Owned by `areas/holding-costs.md`; named here because it is the check on E-R5's "storage fees stop".

R8. **Unfulfillable stock, by disposition and origin.** `getInventorySummaries` with `details=true` (up to 50 SKUs a call), role **AF or PL**: `inventoryDetails.unfulfillableQuantity.*`. Report `GET_FBA_FULFILLMENT_CUSTOMER_RETURNS_DATA`, role **PR or AF**, daily: `detailed-disposition`, `reason`, `status`, `customer-comments`, `license-plate-number`. Ledger `GET_LEDGER_DETAIL_VIEW_DATA` and `GET_LEDGER_SUMMARY_VIEW_DATA`, role **AF only**, 18 months: `Event Type`, `Disposition`, `Reason`, `Reference ID`. MYI reports, role **PR or AF**: `afn-unsellable-quantity`, `afn-listing-exists`. Notification `FBA_INVENTORY_AVAILABILITY_CHANGES`, role **AF**. Pitfalls: value sets undocumented; a repackaged unit moves back to sellable (D5); Amazon may dispose of a unit soon after a return is graded (D6), so a count can fall with no order of the seller's.

R9. **Money from removals and liquidations.** `listFinancialEvents` (Finances v0), role **FA** (also AF in NA and FE): `FBALiquidationEventList` (US only), `RemovalShipmentEventList`, `RemovalShipmentAdjustmentEventList`. `listTransactions` (Finances 2024-06-19), role **FA**; not read for this area. Settlement report `GET_V2_SETTLEMENT_REPORT_DATA_FLAT_FILE_V2`, role **FA**. Data Kiosk Economics, `createQuery`, role **BA** (O8). Reimbursements `GET_FBA_REIMBURSEMENTS_DATA`, role **PR or AF**, belongs to `cx-reimbursement-claims`.

R10. **Recommended removals.** `GET_FBA_RECOMMENDED_REMOVAL_DATA`. Role: **PR or AF**. Daily. Its description still speaks of 365 days and a "Long-Term Storage cleanup event"; whether it returns rows under the current surcharge is untested (O11).

R11. **Write: a removal.** Feed `POST_FLAT_FILE_FBA_CREATE_REMOVAL` through `createFeed`. Role: **AF**. Irreversible in effect: no operation cancels the order, and the cancel window in Seller Central is hours (E-R3). `RemovalDisposition` values undocumented (O3).

R12. **Write: the listing fix.** `patchListingsItem` / `putListingsItem`, role **PL**; `JSON_LISTINGS_FEED`, role PL. The listing playbook owns these.

R13. **Other outlets.** `createFulfillmentOrder` (Multi-Channel Fulfillment), role **AF**: `areas/fbm-and-outside-fba.md`. `getItemEligibilityPreview`, role **AF**, is inbound eligibility, not Outlet or liquidation eligibility.

## Reads that need a role outside the five

- `GET_FLAT_FILE_RETURNS_DATA_BY_RETURN_DATE` (seller-fulfilled returns): Direct-to-Consumer Shipping (Restricted) or Inventory and Order Tracking. It would show returns of seller-fulfilled orders; FBA returns are R8's.
- AWD `listInventory`: Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (NA). It would show stock still upstream of FBA, which bears on whether to release more to an ASIN already in excess.
- `GET_MERCHANT_LISTINGS_ALL_DATA` and `GET_MERCHANT_LISTINGS_INACTIVE_DATA` are reachable with PR or PL and are not outside the five; they are named here only because the mapping also lists Inventory and Order Tracking.

No position rests on these.

## Only in Seller Central

Established by reading the report-type, feed-type, notification-type and role-mapping pages and the local models for any carrier, and finding none.

- S1. **The automated settings and their state**: stranded automatic-action settings (days, return or dispose, opt-outs per listing), Automated unfulfillable settings (return, dispose, liquidate, refurbish, Grade and Resell, schedule, address), Automated fulfillable settings, Returns Evaluation Settings. No operation reads or writes them. They decide what Amazon does with no further notice, so a reader of the API cannot tell from data alone why units left.
- S2. **The stranded reason table** with Amazon's fix for each reason, and the maximum days per reason code (help pages, unread).
- S3. **Creating a liquidation or a disposal by name, and cancelling any removal order.** The feed may or may not cover the first two (O3); nothing covers the third.
- S4. **Outlet eligibility and creation**, the Manage Excess Inventory recommendations, and the FBA Inventory Age & Excess Analytics page. The planning report carries `recommended-action`, which may be the same recommendation ([seat]); Outlet eligibility itself is in no read.
- S5. **Required-removal notices, the 23-day and other notices, and the stranded emails.** They arrive by email and in Seller Central.
- S6. **Whether a disposed unit was donated**, and donation reporting.
- S7. **Liquidation proceeds outside the US**: `FBALiquidationEvent` is US only; where UK and EU proceeds show in the API was not established (O12).

`method/seller-central-only.md` owns the full list.

## The seller's choices

Each is the seller's. What each changes is stated from the sections above; nothing is picked.

1. **Hold.** Storage keeps accruing, age keeps counting toward the surcharge bands (to 2027-02-01 in the US; an age-based storage rate after), and the units keep counting as excess in the IPI if above Amazon's 90 days of supply. Nothing is irreversible. Research: the purchase cost is sunk and plays no part in the price of stock already owned (PR1); starting high can be rational when demand is unknown (PR5).
2. **Fix the listing** (stranded stock). Units become sellable again; Amazon says to allow 48 hours. Leaving it means removal or disposal on the date in the stranded report (A7, A11).
3. **Reprice, coupon, deal, Outlet.** Sells through at a lower price; standard fees still apply; Outlet needs 90 days in FBA and a minimum discount and may be cancelled by Amazon (E-O1). Research: disciplined markdowns gained 4 to 6% of clearance revenue in store apparel, with earlier and shallower cuts doing better than late deep ones (PR3, PR4); none of it was measured on Amazon.
4. **Advertise.** Costs ad spend against units that may or may not move; the ads playbook owns it.
5. **Sell elsewhere from FBA (Multi-Channel Fulfillment).** Units leave without a removal; MCF fees apply (`areas/fbm-and-outside-fba.md`).
6. **Remove to an address.** The seller keeps the goods. A per-unit fee, charged as units leave (E-R4). Capacity is freed within 24 hours of the order; the aged surcharge stops if filed by the cut-off; arrival takes from days to months (E-R2, P4). After an automated removal the ASIN may be barred from inbound for up to 90 days (C4). Units sent back in are a new inbound.
7. **Liquidate.** Amazon states 5-10% gross, less a processing fee and 15%; practitioners report 2-5% net (P4). Fees stop at submission; paid within about 90 days; cannot be cancelled; no restriction on sending the ASIN again; units may reappear for sale by others (Dave Bryant). Excludes hazmat, recalled, face masks, jewellery, expired, defective and warehouse-damaged units.
8. **Dispose.** A per-unit fee; nothing recovered; Amazon donates what it can, and in the US and Canada the seller cannot opt out of that (E-D2). Any tax effect is the seller's adviser's question (PR9).
9. **Grade and Resell** (customer returns only). Returns are sold as used under the seller's control of price; grading takes time and used units sell more slowly (P4, PR6).
10. **Bundle or relabel.** Means a removal and a new inbound under another SKU; practitioners test with about 50 units (P3).
11. **The settings themselves.** Return or dispose for stranded and unfulfillable stock, the days, the schedule, whether liquidation, refurbishment and Grade and Resell are on, and whether aged sellable stock is removed automatically. With none set, Amazon's defaults apply: disposal, and in the US and Canada liquidation and donation. A seasonal seller's automated removal of unsold stock carries the up-to-90-day inbound wait (C4). Disabling automated fulfillable removal does not, on Amazon's latest US statement, stop the removal of sellable stock aged over 270 days; it leaves the disposition to Amazon (C4).
12. **Whether to keep sending the ASIN.** Amazon refuses inbound of a stranded ASIN (A10) and may refuse more of one with high days of supply (B6).

## Positions

Scope is stated where a rule is not worldwide. "Listing seat 2026-10-14" means the help-page quotation in `packages/cx-listing-playbook/research/angles/inventory-and-fulfillment.md`, not re-read here.

- **P-stuck-stock-1.** Amazon defines stranded inventory as FBA inventory in fulfillment centers with no associated active offer, which needs either the listing reactivated or the units removed. Sources: Bryce_Amazon 2024-03-12; *Fix stranded inventory* (listing seat 2026-10-14). §A1.
- **P-stuck-stock-2.** Closing or deleting a listing, changing it from FBA to seller-fulfilled, listing issues, infringement concerns and system errors are the causes Amazon names; the fix depends on the stranded reason, and Amazon's table of reasons lives on a Seller Central help page. Sources: Bryce_Amazon 2024-03-12. §A2, A4.
- **P-stuck-stock-3.** No field of `getInventorySummaries` and no column of the MYI reports counts stranded units; the stranded report `GET_STRANDED_INVENTORY_UI_DATA` (role Pricing or Amazon Fulfillment, near real-time) is the read, with `stranded-reason`, `primary-action`, `date-stranded` and `Date-to-take-auto-removal`. Sources: FBA report type values (2026-10-13); `fbaInventory.json`. §A11, D1, F1, F2.
- **P-stuck-stock-4.** The documented stranded report has a `Date-to-take-auto-removal` column and no `date-classified-as-unsellable` column; since 2020-06-30 aged stranded stock is removed under the seller's settings instead of being reclassified as unsellable. Sources: FBA report type values; News_Amazon 2020-07-02. §A6, A11.
- **P-stuck-stock-5.** Stranded stock that is not fixed is removed or disposed of automatically; the number of days depends on the stranded reason and the seller's setting, with no setting the units are disposed of, and stranded units with an active offer are not auto-removed. Amazon's own texts give different day counts (30 days after notification; the maximum per reason code), so the dated value is the report's column. Sources: *Stranded inventory required removals* (listing seat 2026-10-14); Bryce_Amazon 2024-03-12; News_Amazon 2020-07-02. §A7.
- **P-stuck-stock-6.** Since 2019-06-25 Amazon relists stock stranded as "Listing closed", "Listing closed and inventory error" or "Item discontinued", and switches "Merchant fulfilled" strandings back to FBA, automatically unless the seller opted the listing out or set a future offer start date. Sources: News_Amazon 2019-06-25. §A5.
- **P-stuck-stock-7.** Amazon refuses an inbound shipment that contains an ASIN with stranded units, and says a fix made by the seller can take 48 hours to show in the stranded view. Sources: *Fix stranded inventory* (listing seat 2026-10-14). §A10.
- **P-stuck-stock-8.** Units under the Amazon-created SKUs `Amazon.Found.B…` and `FBA….Missing#` are stranded until the seller creates a listing for them, and are otherwise removed under the auto-removal settings. Sources: *Fix stranded inventory* (listing seat 2026-10-14). §A10.
- **P-stuck-stock-9.** In the UK and the EU the stranded settings, removal dates and quantities are separate between UK-stored and EU-stored stock. Sources: News_Amazon 2021-05-06. §A8.
- **P-stuck-stock-10.** The Bulk Fix Stranded Inventory report (`GET_STRANDED_INVENTORY_LOADER_DATA`) tells the reader to submit it with `POST_FLAT_FILE_INVLOADER_DATA`, a feed type the Feeds API no longer lists; Amazon's deprecation notice exempts uploads made in Seller Central. Sources: FBA report type values; listings-feeds deprecation changelog; Feed Type Values (2026-11-05). §A12.
- **P-stuck-stock-11.** The listing side of a stranding is read from the Listings Items API (`status`, and an issue's enforcement action `LISTING_SUPPRESSED` or `CATALOG_ITEM_REMOVED`; `SEARCH_SUPPRESSED` leaves the offer buyable) and from `GET_MERCHANTS_LISTINGS_FYP_REPORT`; both need Product Listing and neither carries a quantity. Sources: `listingsItems_2021-08-01.json`; Inventory report type values. §F3, F4.
- **P-stuck-stock-12.** Amazon considers an item excess when it has over 90 days of supply on Amazon's forecast of demand; the forecast is not exposed, and the figure is Amazon's definition. Sources: *Inventory performance* (listing seat 2026-10-14); Christine_Amazon 2026-03-10. §B1.
- **P-stuck-stock-13.** `GET_FBA_INVENTORY_PLANNING_DATA` (role Amazon Fulfillment only) is the single successor of the excess, inventory health and inventory age reports removed on 2022-06-27; it carries `estimated-excess-quantity`, `recommended-action`, `recommended-sales-price`, `recommended-sale-duration-days`, `recommended-removal-quantity` and `estimated-cost-savings-of-recommended-actions`, and marks `healthy-inventory-level` deprecated. Sources: FBA report type values; SP-API Deprecation Schedule. §B3, B5.
- **P-stuck-stock-14.** The planning report is not available in the NL, PL, SE and BE stores, returns a reduced set in Brazil, and defines no value set for `recommended-action` or `alert`. Sources: FBA report type values. §B3.
- **P-stuck-stock-15.** The planning report's age columns are fixed bands: two overlapping coarse cuts above 180 days, a finer 0-30/31-60/61-90 cut, and over a year either `inv-age-365-plus-days` or, in the US, DE, UK, FR, IT and ES stores, `inv-age-366-to-455-days` and `inv-age-456-plus-days`; surcharge exposure is in the matching `quantity-to-be-charged-ais-*` and `estimated-ais-*` columns. Sources: FBA report type values. §C1.
- **P-stuck-stock-16.** In the US the aged inventory surcharge is assessed on the 15th of each month, and a removal or disposal order submitted by 11:59 PM PT on the 14th keeps its units out of that month's surcharge even if they have not left; this is an Amazon moderator's statement of 2026-02-09, and the charged amounts are read from the long-term storage fee charges report. Sources: Angie_Amazon 2026-02-09; 船长BI and 赛狐ERP say the same (their accounts). §C2.
- **P-stuck-stock-17.** Amazon has announced that from 2027-02-01 the US aged inventory surcharge is removed and combined with monthly storage into one age-based rate, with higher fees for aged inventory. Sources: Update to US Referral and FBA fees for 2027 (2026-11-05). §C3.
- **P-stuck-stock-18.** Under Automated fulfillable settings Amazon returns, liquidates or disposes of sellable stock past an age threshold, and of ASINs unsold for six or more months that are over 180 days old; an ASIN removed this way can be barred from inbound for up to 90 days. The age threshold Amazon stated was 365 days (US, 2024) and 270 days (UK from 2025-04-04; US from 2025-10-26 per an Amazon email a seller quoted); an Amazon moderator wrote in about July 2026 that sellable stock aged over 270 days is removed even when the setting is "Disabled", with Amazon choosing the disposition. Whether the setting is on by default differs between Amazon's texts. Sources: Arish_Amazon (thread of 2026-06-26); Topher_Amazon 2024-03-07; Angie_Amazon 2026-02-09; Amazon emails via sellers 2025-03-05 and 2025-09-29. §C4.
- **P-stuck-stock-19.** The FBA Inventory API reports unfulfillable stock in six dispositions: customer damaged, warehouse damaged, distributor damaged, carrier damaged, defective and expired (`getInventorySummaries` with details; role Amazon Fulfillment or Product Listing). Sources: `fbaInventory.json`. §D1.
- **P-stuck-stock-20.** "Customer damaged" means a return that cannot be resold as new, which includes an opened package; Amazon does not reimburse it, and the unit stays unfulfillable until removed. Sources: Aria_Amazon 2026-02-19. §D3.
- **P-stuck-stock-21.** Amazon repackages eligible returns automatically and the seller cannot turn that off; refurbishment is optional; the returns report shows the statuses "Pending Repackaging" and "Repackaged Successfully". Sources: Indy_Amazon 2026-07-11; News_Amazon 2021-09-09. §D5.
- **P-stuck-stock-22.** In the US, unfulfillable stock with no return option set is disposed of at Amazon's discretion, including immediately after a return is evaluated (from 2023-11-01); keeping a removal schedule requires the return option and a valid address. Sources: News_Amazon 2023-09-08; Topher_Amazon 2024-03-07; Angie_Amazon 2026-04-01. §D6.
- **P-stuck-stock-23.** In the UK and the EU, from 2024-09-16, unfulfillable stock with no automated setting is disposed of or donated after 23 days' notice, and that disposal order cannot be cancelled. Sources: News_Amazon 2024-09-03. §D7.
- **P-stuck-stock-24.** Amazon removes expiry-dated units ahead of the date (a moderator gives 50 days in North America and 45 in the EU), treats best-by, use-by and sell-by dates as expiry dates, and does not return units marked for disposal; Amazon's help page, as restated by a third party in 2024, gives the same 50 days and over 90 days of shelf life at receipt, and says a small number of consumable categories differ without naming them. Sources: Angie_Amazon 2026-03-31; *Expiration dates on FBA products* via ScanPower 2024-09-10. §D9, D9a.
- **P-stuck-stock-25.** Amazon sets a yearly meltable window in the US (2026: no meltable inbound from April 20 to September 28; stock remaining may be marked unfulfillable and disposed of for a fee from May 1). Sources: News_Amazon 2026-03-09. §D10.
- **P-stuck-stock-26.** For stock lost or damaged by Amazon before a customer order, reimbursement is at manufacturing cost from 2025-03-31; removal claims are filed manually within their own windows; the claim itself is outside this pack. Sources: News_Amazon 2024-12-18, 2025-02-28, 2024-10-10. §D12.
- **P-stuck-stock-27.** Amazon's own figures for removal processing range from 5-14 days to "90 days or more" at peak, plus carrier time, and removal orders are worked behind customer orders in peak season; the time an order took is read from the removal order and shipment reports. Sources: TaylorR_Amazon 2025-12-05; Jameson_Amazon 2025-09-18; Angie_Amazon 2026-02-09; News_Amazon (EU) 2021-10-12. Practitioners report up to seven months. §E-R2, P4.
- **P-stuck-stock-28.** A removal order can be cancelled only while in Planning status, which lasts hours; an order Amazon creates (required removal, automated removal, disposal after notice) cannot be cancelled; no API operation cancels a removal. Sources: Dominic_Amazon 2024-10-23; Angie_Amazon 2026-04-01; News_Amazon 2024-09-03. §E-R3.
- **P-stuck-stock-29.** Removal and disposal fees are charged per unit as each unit is removed or disposed of, for orders created from 2026-03-01 in the US and from 2026-05-01 in the UK; rates did not change. Sources: News_Amazon 2026-01-29; News_Amazon (UK) 2026-04-09. §E-R4.
- **P-stuck-stock-30.** A removal order counts against capacity usage within 24 hours of creation even while pending, and units pending removal, disposal or liquidation are left out of historical days of supply. Sources: *FBA capacity limits* and *Low-inventory-level fee* (listing seat 2026-10-14). §E-R5.
- **P-stuck-stock-31.** `POST_FLAT_FILE_FBA_CREATE_REMOVAL` is a live feed type in 2026 (role Amazon Fulfillment; NA, EU, FE), with sandbox scenarios added on 2026-09-30; Amazon's page does not list the allowed `RemovalDisposition` values. Sources: FBA feed type values (2026-10-14); Feed Type Values (2026-11-05); SP-API release notes. §E-R7.
- **P-stuck-stock-32.** The removal order detail report gives, per order line, requested, cancelled, disposed, shipped and in-process quantities and the removal fee; the removal shipment report excludes cancelled and disposed items (both role Pricing or Amazon Fulfillment). Sources: FBA report type values. §E-R6.
- **P-stuck-stock-33.** Amazon states that liquidators pay a gross recovery value typically 5-10% of the product's value, from which Amazon takes a per-item processing fee and 15%; payment arrives within about 90 days; storage and aged surcharges stop at submission; the order cannot be cancelled.
