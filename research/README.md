# Amazon stock playbook: research

This directory is the research base for a skill pack that sits over `cx-amazon-sp-api`, ComChatX's open door to Amazon's Selling Partner API (`cx raw amazon-sp-api`). Its brief is 2026-11-03-307 (the stock playbook, first half): map the whole of the job of a seller's Amazon stock (where every unit is, what holding it costs, when more must land), with Amazon's own words first, practitioners and paid tools as opinion, and published research where there is any. Eleven research files, each written by a research seat and then checked by a verifier seat, plus one ownership file by the lead, were read on 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10. Sources rank in one order: Amazon's own pages, models, forum announcements and staff posts **[official]**; Amazon's words seen only through a third party's verbatim quotation **[official, via <who>]** or only in a search result **[snippet]**; named practitioners and tools **[opinion]**; published research **[research]**. A **[seat]** line in a file is the writing seat's own reading and never becomes a position here.

**No Seller Central help page could be read first-hand.** A plain GET of `sellercentral.amazon.*/help/hub/reference/external/<ID>` (with `?locale=en-US`, on the `.com`, `.co.uk` and other twins) serves a script shell; the help hub's content call is a `POST` to an Amazon host, which the brief rules out, and the lead ruled on 2026-11-10 that it stays off limits. Every fact that comes from a help page is therefore tagged by the route it came through: the listing playbook's quotation of 2026-10-14 (cited as that seat's read, not re-read), the office's captures of 2026-10-14, a forum post that quotes it, or a third party's verbatim copy. The ids are listed at the end of this page with what rests on each. What was kept out, by rule: no Selling Partner API host, no `api.amazon.com`, no `sellingpartner-ai.amazon.com`, no `POST` to any Amazon host, and no seller's data anywhere in the files.

**Office fact, 2026-11-10:** the Amazon Fulfillment role is present on the test store's SP-API application (a `getItemEligibilityPreview` call was answered 200 that day; office-reported, one call on the test store). The door's application is taken to hold five roles: Product Listing (PL), Pricing (PR), Brand Analytics (BA), Finance and Accounting (FA) and Amazon Fulfillment (AF). A read that needs any other role (Amazon Warehousing and Distribution, Selling Partner Insights, Inventory and Order Tracking, Direct-to-Consumer Shipping, Tax roles, Selling Partner Support) is named with what it would open, and no position rests on it.

## How to read this

- **This page is the entry point.** It says what each file covers, what the pack would hold, every position the skills will take, where the files disagreed and how that was settled, where Amazon disagrees with itself, which values are looked up rather than carried, what is still open, and what the door lacks. The files hold the evidence: quotations, column names, model pointers, dates and the pages behind them.
- **Positions are facts, not instructions.** Each states what Amazon, a practitioner, a tool or a study says or does, with the P-ids it merges and its sources, Amazon's first. Under decision D233 none tells cx when to ask the seller, in what order to work, what number to treat as a rule, or what not to read or change: the model judges those from the facts. How a seller forecasts, how much cover they keep and when they ship is the seller's; a practitioner's or tool's number carries its name, and Amazon's figures (28, 90, 181 days and the rest) read as Amazon's dated definitions for the stores named, with the store's own value read where *Read at* says.
- **P-ids** are the files' own: `P-stock-states-7` is position 7 of `areas/stock-states.md`. The tag at the end of each position is that of its weakest load-bearing source; the full citation, URL and read date sit in the file.
- **Figures that move** (fees, limits, dates, rates, column lists) are stated as read on 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10 so a reader knows what to look for. The live value is read at run time: each position whose value differs by store or year says beside it, under *Read at*, which operation, report column or page gives it for the seller's own store, and *Values looked up, not carried* below gathers them, with the ones nothing readable gives.
- **What this synthesis read.** Every file's *Positions* as the verifier left them, *Open questions*, *Reads that need a role outside the five* and *Verification* in full; the *Stable enough*, run-time and *Pages read* sections where a position needed them; and `method/one-fact-one-home.md` in full. It reopened no page. One position was removed by a verifier (`P-seller-central-only-14`); it stays out.

## The pack this research proposes

This is the lead's proposal, written out with the files that feed each part.

**Package name proposed: `@comchatx/cx-stock-playbook`** (the survey's name; it pairs with `cx-listing-playbook` and `cx-sponsored-ads-playbook`).

**One job:** the seller's Amazon stock, where every unit is, what it costs to hold, and when more must land.

### Skills

Seven skills, each a `SKILL.md` of at most 371 words with the rest in `references/`. A skill sentence states a rule and where its value is read for the seller's own store, never a value that differs by store or changes by year (brief 307 correction 1; see *Values looked up, not carried*).

- **`stock-position`** (W, with A, G and F's reads): every state and what can be bought now, the reports and the ledger, where they do not tie, pooled stores (Pan-EU, Remote Fulfillment), AWD as a gap on the five roles, freshness. Fed by `areas/stock-states.md`, `method/sp-api-stock-surface.md`, `beyond/programs-and-regions.md` (sections A–D, J, K) and `areas/fbm-and-outside-fba.md` (its quantity reads).
- **`stock-cost`** (C, with G): every holding charge, its rule, its dated history and its differences by store, the 2027 US change, where each is read at run time; rates never stated. Fed by `areas/holding-costs.md` and `beyond/programs-and-regions.md` (CEP, peak fee, AWD waivers, New Selection).
- **`stock-out`** (O, with W): what out of stock is in Amazon's data, Amazon's stated consequences, what is and is not measured about lasting effects, censored demand and how tools correct it. Fed by `areas/running-out.md`, with `areas/stock-states.md` and `beyond/sellers-tools-and-research.md` (the research and the "14 days").
- **`restock`** (R, with T): Amazon's two recommendation reports and what moves them, the seller settings the API cannot see, lead time and its stages, the bounds Amazon sets on a plan, and the methods as the seller's choices. Fed by `areas/restock-and-forecast.md` and `beyond/sellers-tools-and-research.md`.
- **`stock-inbound`** (I, with T where it bears): inbound read as a position: what is on its way, in what state, when it becomes sellable, what went missing and where the claim line falls; the writes the grant carries, with what is permanent and what each charges, stated as facts; and where this pack ends and an inbound-shipments plugin would begin. Fed by `areas/inbound.md`, with `beyond/programs-and-regions.md` for the peak cut-offs and AWD inbound.
- **`stuck-stock`** (S): stranded, excess, aged and unsellable stock, and every way out with what is permanent. Fed by `areas/stuck-stock.md`.
- **`fbm-and-channels`** (F): FBM quantity reads and writes, an ASIN with both offers, Multi-Channel Fulfillment as a drain on FBA stock, stock held outside Amazon. Fed by `areas/fbm-and-outside-fba.md`.

### Script

None. `method/sp-api-stock-surface.md` and its verifier found no step cx cannot write itself with a shell and `cx raw`: the report create-poll-download loop, decompression, TSV parsing, ledger slices, Data Kiosk queries and inventory paging. A script would be justified only if a first run shows cx cannot hold a create-poll-download loop in one turn. The verifier's other side, recorded as the seats' own: `getInventorySummaries`' 30-second page token means paging works only inside one shell loop written before the first page (or by `sellerSkus` in batches of 50, or the MYI report instead); past the burst of 15, `createReport` runs one a minute, so a five-store, ten-report job is about 35 minutes of waiting; the five-minute document URL needs create, poll and download chained without a model turn between `getReportDocument` and the fetch on a slow day. Each is a loop cx writes, not an inability; whether such a loop survives a cx turn is untested.

### W. Where every unit is (`stock-position`)

- **W1.** Amazon names five states for FBA stock: fulfillable ("can be picked, packed, and shipped"), inbound, reserved, unfulfillable ("cannot be sold") and researching; reserved, inbound, researching and unfulfillable are separate fields beside the fulfillable quantity. — `P-stock-states-1`, `P-running-out-1`, `P-running-out-2`. [official] *FBA Inventory API* guide; `fbaInventory.json`; notification schema; *Inventory Buyability* GFC63UNUF6LMLK2R (listing seat 2026-10-14).
- **W2.** `getInventorySummaries` returns one summary per seller SKU for one marketplace; its `totalQuantity` is "the total number of units in an inbound shipment or in Amazon fulfillment centers", so it includes inbound; Amazon's guide says the API "is available in all marketplaces". — `P-stock-states-2`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-6`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`; *FBA Inventory API* guide (2026-09-09).
- **W3.** The quantity breakdown is returned only when the request sets `details=true` (default false); a request takes one marketplace and at most 50 SKUs, at 2 requests a second with a burst of 2; the roles Amazon names are Amazon Fulfillment or Product Listing (the *Get inventory summaries* tutorial names Product Listing only). — `P-stock-states-3`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-1`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-2`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`; *Get inventory summaries*; *Role Mappings*.
- **W4.** A request with `startDateTime` returns summaries changed since then, reaches back at most 18 months, ignores the SKU filters, and does not detect changes in the three inbound quantities. — `P-stock-states-20`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-3`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`.
- **W5.** A `getInventorySummaries` `nextToken` expires 30 seconds after it is created, a `startDateTime` request must repeat `startDateTime` with every token, and an empty page that still carries a token is expected (Amazon support, as quoted by a developer); the `searchOrders` token, by contrast, lives 24 hours. — `P-stock-states-21`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-2`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`; `orders_2026-01-01.json`; [opinion] GitHub issue #1983.
- **W6.** Amazon defines `fulfillableQuantity` as "The item quantity that can be picked, packed, and shipped"; the API's only granularity is the marketplace, where it is the stock that "could be fulfilled in the given marketplace", not the stock physically in that country. — `P-stock-states-17`, `P-running-out-1`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-4`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`.
- **W7.** Amazon defines inbound working as units in a shipment "for which you have notified Amazon", inbound shipped as units for which the seller has also "provided a tracking number", and inbound receiving as units not yet received in a shipment some of whose units have been; the three are per SKU, not per shipment, and appear as `afn-inbound-working/shipped/receiving-quantity` in the MYI report and as `Working`, `Shipped`, `Receiving` in the Restock report, whose `Total Days of Supply` includes units from open shipments. — `P-stock-states-4`, `P-inbound-10`, `P-inbound-11`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`; *FBA Reports*.
- **W8.** By shipment status, Amazon's notification schema counts WORKING shipments as Working and SHIPPED, IN_TRANSIT, DELIVERED and CHECKED_IN shipments as Shipped; Receiving begins only when units "are in progress to be received". 赛狐 instead groups DELIVERED and CHECKED_IN under receiving (its mapping). — `P-stock-states-5`. [official] `FBAInventoryAvailabilityChangeNotification.json`; [opinion] 赛狐.
- **W9.** In the API, reserved has three parts: `pendingCustomerOrderQuantity` (reserved for customer orders, including orders placed but not yet authorised), `pendingTransshipmentQuantity` (moving between fulfilment centres) and `fcProcessingQuantity` (sidelined for additional processing). — `P-stock-states-6`, `P-running-out-36`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`; Orders v0 model.
- **W10.** Whether FC-transfer units can be bought is stated two ways by Amazon: an Amazon moderator wrote on 2024-08-26 that they "are available for customers to buy", possibly with a future ship date, citing the Reserved Inventory report help page (G201723860, unread), while the *Inventory Buyability* help page says customers cannot buy reserved units; the MYI reports carry `afn-fc-transfer-quantity` and `afn-onhand-buyable-quantity`, undefined on the page. The buyable statement is one moderator's post. — `P-stock-states-7`, `P-running-out-58`. [official] Topher_Amazon, seller forums; *FBA Reports*; *Inventory Buyability* GFC63UNUF6LMLK2R (listing seat 2026-10-14).
- **W11.** FC processing covers, in Amazon's words, "verification of item dimensions and weight, pending investigations, or pending removal orders". — `P-stock-states-8`. [official] Topher_Amazon 2024-08-26; [official, via 领星] Amazon's Chinese column text.
- **W12.** The API's reserved total includes units in FC transfer by definition, while 领星 and 积加 describe Seller Central's reserved figure as customer orders plus FC processing with FC transfer outside it, and DataHawk reports that the planning report includes FC transfer in reserved only in the IN, SA, AE, BR and AU stores; Amazon has not published which holds per report. — `P-stock-states-9`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`; [opinion] 领星, 积加, DataHawk (2026-06-01).
- **W13.** Amazon's page lists the reserved report's (`GET_RESERVED_INVENTORY_DATA`) columns as `reserved_qty`, `reserved_customerorders`, `reserved_fc-processing` and `reserved_staging` (US, CA, JP, UK, DE, FR, IT, ES), with no FC-transfer column; developers report that the reserved, planning and MYI stock columns changed between June and September 2026 with no release note, and on one point (MYI FC-transfer and on-hand-buyable columns gone on 2026-09-07) their report and Amazon's list of 2026-10-13 disagree. The dated changes are developers' word; a file's own header row states what it contains. — `P-stock-states-10`, `P-stock-states-11`. [official] *FBA Reports*; [opinion] GitHub issues #5283, #5284, #5289.
- **W14.** Researching is, in Amazon's definition, "misplaced or warehouse damaged units that are actively being confirmed", aged by the API in three buckets: 1–10 days, 11–20 days, and 21 days or longer. — `P-stock-states-12`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`.
- **W15.** Amazon's sources give two limits for researching: its 2021 launch text, as a seller quoted it, says the research "will never exceed 30 days" (and the Chinese text via 领星 says the same), and the English *Inventory Buyability* page says "up to 60 days"; the 2021 text also says that once researched units are reimbursed or the period ends the adjustment moves to the Inventory Adjustment report, which the Inventory Ledger has since replaced. *Read at:* how long the seller's units have been researching: `getInventorySummaries` `researchingQuantityBreakdown` (1–10, 11–20, 21+ days); the limit itself: *Inventory Buyability* GFC63UNUF6LMLK2R (unreachable). — `P-stock-states-13`, `P-stock-states-51`. [official, via a seller's forum post, 2021-04-07]; [official, via 领星]; *Inventory Buyability* (listing seat 2026-10-14); News_Amazon 2022-10-24.
- **W16.** Unfulfillable has six dispositions in the API: customer damaged, warehouse damaged, distributor damaged, carrier damaged, defective and expired (read with `details=true`). — `P-stock-states-14`, `P-stuck-stock-19`. [official] `fbaInventory.json`.
- **W17.** Units Amazon offers ahead of receipt through In-Stock Head Start are `FutureSupplyBuyable` (findable and buyable) and `PendingCustomerOrderInTransit` (already bought in transit) in `FBA_INVENTORY_AVAILABILITY_CHANGES`, and `afn-future-supply-buyable` and `afn-reserved-future-supply` in the MYI reports; `getInventorySummaries` has no such field. Units sold this way are still inside inbound, so inbound is not always unsold stock. — `P-stock-states-15`, `P-stock-states-16`, `P-running-out-10`. [official] notification schema; *FBA Reports*; `fbaInventory.json`; News_Amazon 2019-07-25.
- **W18.** Two developers report (2023-09-22, 2024-04-11) that under Pan-European FBA `getInventorySummaries` returns the same sum for each participating EU marketplace (the UK excluded), so adding those marketplaces multiplies the stock; Amazon's guide does not mention it. Scope: EU stores. — `P-stock-states-18`. [official] `fbaInventory.json` (the granularity definition); [opinion] GitHub issues #452, #360; Improvado.
- **W19.** `GET_AFN_INVENTORY_DATA_BY_COUNTRY` gives the quantity available for local fulfilment per country, is offered for Europe only, and carries no reserved, inbound or unfulfillable figure; movement between European countries is read from the Inventory Ledger, and the Seller Central "Cross-Border Inventory Movement report" has no documented SP-API report type. Scope: Europe. — `P-stock-states-19`, `P-programs-and-regions-6`. [official] *FBA Reports*; *Inventory Reports*; *Role Mappings*.
- **W20.** Amazon states that a near real-time FBA report is regenerated at most once every 30 minutes and a daily one at most once every four hours, that a request inside the window returns the copy already made, and that these intervals do not limit how often a report may be requested. — `P-stock-states-22`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-20`, `P-restock-and-forecast-43`. [official] *FBA Reports*.
- **W21.** `GET_FBA_MYI_UNSUPPRESSED_INVENTORY_DATA` covers active listings and `GET_FBA_MYI_ALL_INVENTORY_DATA` adds archived ones; both are current snapshots with no history, and Amazon's SP-API page defines none of their columns. — `P-stock-states-23`. [official] *FBA Reports*; [opinion] Improvado.
- **W22.** `GET_AFN_INVENTORY_DATA` splits a SKU's available quantity by warehouse condition code and requires the Amazon Fulfillment role alone. — `P-stock-states-24`. [official] *FBA Reports*; *Role Mappings*.
- **W23.** `GET_FBA_INVENTORY_PLANNING_DATA` (the page's heading: "FBA Manage Inventory Health Report"; Amazon Fulfillment only) is not available in the NL, PL, SE and BE stores, has a reduced column set in Brazil, states no update frequency, and carries three dates on one row (`snapshot-date`, `Inventory age snapshot date`, `Last updated date for Historical Days of Supply`), so its figures are not all of one day. — `P-stock-states-25`, `P-restock-and-forecast-7`, `P-restock-and-forecast-9`, `P-stuck-stock-14`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-25`, `P-programs-and-regions-17`. [official] *FBA Reports*; *Role Mappings*.
- **W24.** Developers have reported the planning report omitting part of an account's SKUs on some runs while the MYI report stayed complete, and a seller states it omits out-of-stock listings (Amazon did not dispute it in that thread). — `P-stock-states-26`, `P-restock-and-forecast-45`. [opinion] GitHub issue #5044 (2025-11-19); a seller in KJ_Amazon's thread f4dc91de….
- **W25.** `GET_FBA_FULFILLMENT_INVENTORY_HEALTH_DATA`, `GET_EXCESS_INVENTORY_DATA` and `GET_FBA_INVENTORY_AGED_DATA` were removed on 2022-06-27, six inventory history, adjustment, receipt and reconciliation report types on 2023-01-31, and `GET_FBA_UNO_INVENTORY_DATA` on 2024-03-27; the Inventory Ledger replaced the six (Amazon first announced their end for 2022-09-30, then for 2023-01-31). — `P-stock-states-27`, `P-stock-states-28`. [official] *SP-API Deprecation Schedule*; News_Amazon 2022-08-02, 2022-10-24.
- **W26.** The ledger summary balances a period as starting balance, receipts, customer shipments, customer returns, vendor returns, transfers, found, lost, damaged, disposed, other and unknown events, and ending balance, per FNSKU and disposition, by country (default) or fulfilment centre (`aggregateByLocation`) and by month (default), week or day (`aggregatedByTimePeriod`); a request whose dates do not match the period (a whole month, a whole week) "can return empty data". — `P-stock-states-29`, `P-stock-states-32`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-23`. [official] *FBA Reports*.
- **W27.** The ledger detail lists each movement for 18 months with event type (Adjustments, CustomerReturns, Receipts, Shipments, VendorReturns, WhseTransfers), fulfilment centre, date, disposition, reason, and reconciled and unreconciled quantity, filterable by `eventType`; receipts per fulfilment centre and date sit under `Receipts`. — `P-stock-states-30`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-24`, `P-inbound-31`. [official] *FBA Reports*.
- **W28.** The ledger carries no inbound quantity and keeps units moving between fulfilment centres in a column ("In Transit Between Warehouses") separate from the ending warehouse balance; Amazon, asked on its forum whether to add the two, gave no rule. ### C. What holding stock costs (`stock-cost`)

- **C1.** Monthly storage is charged on the daily average volume a seller's units occupy in a calendar month, by size class and by season (January–September, October–December), on Amazon's own measurement of the packaged unit; the month's fee is charged in the following month, and the storage fee report (`GET_FBA_STORAGE_FEE_CHARGES_DATA`, Amazon Fulfillment or Pricing) gives it as an estimate per FNSKU per fulfilment centre with the base rate and the utilization surcharge in separate columns. *Read at:* the rate applied to the seller's units: `GET_FBA_STORAGE_FEE_CHARGES_DATA` columns `storage_rate`, `base_rate`, `country_code`, `month_of_charge`; the published table: US help page G3EDYEF6KUCFQTNM (unreachable), the Europe rate card PDF linked from each `sell.amazon.<tld>/pricing` page (2 Nov 2026 edition: https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/261014-FBA-Rate-Card-EN.pdf), the store's pricing page (`sell.amazon.ca/pricing`, `sell.amazon.co.jp/pricing`, `sell.amazon.com.au/pricing`, `sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing`, https://vender.amazon.com.mx/precios, `sell.amazon.ae`, `sell.amazon.sa`, `sell.amazon.in`). — `P-holding-costs-1`, `P-holding-costs-2`. [official] EU rate card 2 Nov 2026 p.21 and FAQ Q2; sell.amazon.ca, .com.au, .co.jp, vender.amazon.com.mx pricing pages; *FBA Reports*; [snippet] US help page for the 7th–15th window.
- **C2.** Stranded stock keeps paying storage; units under a submitted removal or liquidation order stop accruing storage and the aged surcharge (US; staff posts, not a policy page; sellers dispute that storage stops at creation in every case). — `P-holding-costs-3`. [official] Kris_Amazon 2026-10-06; Angie_Amazon 2026-02-09.
- **C3.** Amazon defines the storage utilization ratio as average daily volume stored over average daily volume shipped in the past 13 weeks, in weeks, per seller per size class on the last day of the month; it is not a per-SKU number, and the storage fee report carries `storage_utilization_ratio`, `storage_utilization_ratio_units`, `utilization_surcharge_rate` and `est_sus`. - **C4.** The US storage utilization surcharge began 2023-04-01 and ends 2027-01-31; Canada's began 2023-09-01 and ends the same day; Europe's began 2023-05-01 and Japan's 2024-06-01, with no end announced by 2026-11-10. *Read at:* whether the surcharge still applies in a month: `GET_FBA_STORAGE_FEE_CHARGES_DATA` (`utilization_surcharge_rate`, `est_sus`); the US and Canadian end: *Update to US Referral and Fulfillment by Amazon fees for 2027*, https://sellingpartners.aboutamazon.com/update-to-us-referral-and-fulfillment-by-amazon-fees-for-2027 and https://globalselling.amazon.com/news/news-notics-261105. — `P-holding-costs-5`. [research] Teunter, Syntetos & Babai 2011.

### S. Stuck stock and the ways out (`stuck-stock`)

- **S1.** Amazon defines stranded inventory as FBA inventory in fulfilment centres with no associated active offer, needing either the listing reactivated or the units removed; Amazon names closing or deleting a listing, changing it from FBA to seller-fulfilled, listing issues, infringement concerns and system errors as causes, says stranding "is usually due to a listing or account health issue", and keeps its table of reasons on a help page; no text read names the reason code an account-health stranding carries. — `P-stuck-stock-1`, `P-stuck-stock-2`, `P-stuck-stock-47`. [official] Bryce_Amazon 2024-03-12; *Fix stranded inventory* and *Inventory Buyability* (listing seat 2026-10-14).
- **S2.** No field of `getInventorySummaries` and no MYI column counts stranded units; the stranded report `GET_STRANDED_INVENTORY_UI_DATA` (Pricing or Amazon Fulfillment, near real-time) is the read, with `stranded-reason`, `primary-action`, `date-stranded` and `Date-to-take-auto-removal` and no `date-classified-as-unsellable`, since from 2020-06-30 aged stranded stock is removed under the seller's settings instead of being reclassified as unsellable. — `P-stuck-stock-3`, `P-stuck-stock-4`. [official] *FBA Reports*; `fbaInventory.json`; News_Amazon 2020-07-02.
- **S3.** Stranded stock that is not fixed is removed or disposed of automatically; the days depend on the stranded reason and the seller's setting, with no setting the units are disposed of, and stranded units with an active offer are not auto-removed; Amazon's texts give different day counts (30 days after notification; a maximum per reason code), so the dated value is the report's column. *Read at:* per SKU, the reason and the removal date: `GET_STRANDED_INVENTORY_UI_DATA` (`stranded-reason`, `date-stranded`, `Date-to-take-auto-removal`); the rule: *Stranded inventory required removals* (unreachable help page). — `P-stuck-stock-5`. ### F. FBM quantity and stock outside FBA (`fbm-and-channels`)

- **F1.** The Listings Items API holds two copies of a seller-fulfilled quantity: the `fulfillment_availability` attribute is the seller's last submission, and the `fulfillmentAvailability` data set is "the live quantity available for purchase and source of truth for actual inventory levels"; Product Listing reaches both. — `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-1`, `P-sp-api-stock-surface-15`. [official] *Understanding Amazon listing status and seller-fulfilled inventory management*; *Listings Items API*; `listingsItems_2021-08-01.json`.
- **F2.** Amazon decrements a seller-fulfilled SKU's available quantity when an order is placed, changes it again when an order is cancelled, and stops taking orders at zero. — `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-2`. [official] *Notification Type Values*, `LISTINGS_ITEM_MFN_QUANTITY_CHANGE`.
- **F3.** `fulfillment_availability` is an array of records keyed by `fulfillment_channel_code`: `DEFAULT` is the seller-fulfilled channel, `AMAZON_NA`, `AMAZON_EU`, `AMAZON_JP` and `AMAZON_IN` are FBA, and the codes valid for a seller and store are read from the product type schema; `quantity` "is supported for MFN fulfillment, but not for Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) fulfillment". — `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-3`, `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-4`. [official] *Manage Amazon Haul, Advanced Multiple-Offer, and Multiple-Fulfillment Use Cases*; *Additional functionality (Fulfillment Inbound)*.
- **F4.** A patch with `op: merge` on `/attributes/fulfillment_availability` changes `quantity` for the named channel (the channel code given as the selector) and leaves `restock_date` and `lead_time_to_ship_max_days` as they were; only top-level attributes can be patched, so a `replace` submits the whole array; `merge` has been available for this attribute since 2025-05-28 and only for it and `purchasable_offer`. — `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-5`, `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-6`. [official] *Merge a listing*; `listingsItems_2021-08-01.json`; *Listings Items API*.
- **F5.** A quantity write can use the product type `PRODUCT`, and both `patchListingsItem` and `JSON_LISTINGS_FEED` are open to Product Listing; `JSON_LISTINGS_FEED` takes up to 25,000 SKUs a feed and five feeds every five minutes. — `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-7`, `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-11`. [official] *Listings Items API*; *Submit Listings data*; *Listings APIs FAQ*; *Role Mappings*.
- **F6.** A submission status of `ACCEPTED` means the write was taken for processing; issues that arise afterwards are read from `getListingsItem`, which Amazon says reflects a change "within 1-5 minutes", while listing status and issues notifications normally lag 15–20 minutes. — `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-8`, `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-9`. [official] *Listings Items API*; *Listings APIs FAQ*.
- **F7.** Amazon orders a patch and a feed by submission time, with the feed's submission time applied to every message in it, so an earlier-submitted feed does not overwrite a later patch. — `P-fbm-and-outside-fba-10`. [official] *Listings APIs FAQ*.

### The seat's own (no source; enters no skill)

## What `cx-amazon-sp-api` lacks for this job

Read against the door's `SKILL.md` and `references/door.md` (PR #5 head `dd0343dc`, as read by the lead and the surface file on 2026-11-10). Each is a follow-up offered to that package; none is done here, and none changes a position.

1. **Report generation intervals.** The door states report retention (90 days), the document URL's five minutes and the `GET_MERCHANT_LISTINGS_ALL_DATA` same-document hour, but not Amazon's FBA rule that a near real-time report is regenerated at most every 30 minutes and a daily one every four hours (W20), nor the per-type request limits on the FBA page (the fee preview report once a day, C38).
2. **`reportOptions` per stock type.** The door names `reportOptions` for SQP only; the ledger summary's `aggregateByLocation` and `aggregatedByTimePeriod`, and the whole-month or whole-week date rule that otherwise returns empty data (W26), are not named.
3. **Encoding and time zones.** Amazon states no character encoding and no time zone for any FBA report (A2); a developer saw cp-1252 where UTF-8 was expected. The door says nothing of either.
4. **The 30-second inventory page token.** `getInventorySummaries`' `nextToken` expires 30 seconds after it is created (W5); every `cx raw` is a separate process with its own token trade, so paging works only inside one shell loop. The door does not name the token's life or the `sellerSkus` batches of 50 that avoid it.
5. **Rate discrepancies.** Amazon's inbound rate page and release note give 5 a second for six inbound reads where the model gives 2 (I2); the door says rates are "stated in each model".
6. **Roles that the role page omits or disputes.** Fulfillment Outbound v2026-07-04 has no section on the operations role page (F21); two FBA reports' roles differ between Amazon's two role sources (W46); the AWD replenishment-order and outbound operations have no role anywhere (W38). The door's role list does not say where Amazon's pages leave a gap.
7. **AWD.** The door's five roles do not reach AWD; the door does not say AWD stock is unreadable without a sixth role, nor that the AWD API is US-only.
8. **Stock notifications.** `FBA_INVENTORY_AVAILABILITY_CHANGES` and `LISTINGS_ITEM_MFN_QUANTITY_CHANGE` reach a subscriber only through Amazon SQS or EventBridge (W33, F12); the door has no destination, so a stock job polls.
9. **Column drift.** Amazon retains deprecated columns and adds columns without notice, and developers report unannounced stock-column changes in 2026 (A3, W13); a reader of a stock report takes column names from the header row, which the door does not say.
10. **Freshness of the reads it names.** The planning report and the ledger pair state no freshness and no request limit on Amazon's page (W23); listing reports run 1 to 6 hours old (F10); `ITEM_INVENTORY_EVENT_CHANGE` lags up to 24 hours (W34). None is in the door.
