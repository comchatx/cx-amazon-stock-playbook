## What this covers

- Europe: Pan-European FBA, the European Fulfilment Network (EFN), the Central Europe Programme (CEP), Multi-Country Inventory (MCI), the UK after Brexit (Remote Fulfilment and local inbounding), Amazon.ie, Northern Ireland, GPSR and EPR as reasons stock stops selling, and the reads that show stock by country and its movement across borders.
- North America: Remote Fulfillment with FBA (NARF) to Canada, Mexico and Brazil; the North America and Brazil unified account; own stock in Canada, Mexico and Brazil.
- Other stores (Japan, Australia, UAE, Saudi Arabia, India, Singapore, Brazil, Türkiye, Egypt, South Africa): what Amazon's pages say about stock there, and which SP-API reads are missing or reduced where.
- Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD): where it runs, how stock gets in, replenishment to FBA, the benefits Amazon attaches with their conditions and dates, the quantities shown, the AWD API, Multichannel Distribution; Global Warehousing and Distribution (GWD).
- Upstream logistics: Supply Chain by Amazon / Amazon Supply Chain Services, Amazon Global Logistics (AGL), Amazon SEND, the Partner Carrier program (PCP), Amazon Freight, as they bear on where stock is and how it is seen.
- Subscribe & Save: its stock conditions and what the Replenishment API returns.
- Special-rule stock: dangerous goods, meltables, expiry and lot, storage types, extra-large, commingling and barcodes, Small and Light and its successor, FBA Export, the New Selection program, FBA Onsite / Seller Flex.
- The season: inbound cut-offs for 2023 to 2026, the peak fee period, capacity-limit timing, removal and receiving slowdowns, the holiday returns window, shipment auto-closure.
- Vendor and hybrid: one short pointer.

## Amazon's own rules

### A. Pan-European FBA (stores: DE, FR, IT, ES, NL, PL, SE, BE, IE)

Sources: *Pan-European FBA* program page, https://sell.amazon.co.uk/fulfilment-by-amazon/fba-europe (UK-PANEU); the handbook PDF *Pan-EU_EN*, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/FBA_SSR_Contents/Pan-EU_EN.pdf (carries 2022 fee examples); News_Amazon posts on sellercentral.amazon.co.uk; the *Europe Fees* rate card "Effective as of 2 November, 2026", https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/261014-FBA-Rate-Card-EN.pdf (RC-EU). All [official].

- **What the seller grants.** "When you enable countries for inventory placement, you are giving Amazon permission to store your FBA inventory in any of those countries. Amazon may then move your inventory between EU fulfillment centers at its own discretion and at any time, at no additional cost to you." (UK-PANEU, FAQ). "Once you send your products to an EU fulfillment center, we will store your inventory across our EU fulfillment network in the countries that you have enabled for inventory placement."
- **Storage countries the seller can enable.** "Enable at least 2 countries for Amazon to store your products* (France, Germany, Italy, Spain or Poland)" and "*By storing goods in countries other than your home store, you will have additional VAT reporting requirements." (UK-PANEU, step 3). Benefit as Amazon words it: "You can start with just two VAT numbers, which means you won't need to register for VAT in every country where you sell."
- **Stores an offer must be live in.** "List your FBA products with the same SKU in all required Amazon EU stores, Germany, France, Italy, Spain and the Netherlands*." "*Starting 03 September 2026, all Pan-EU FBA products (new and existing) must have an active Netherlands offer to enrol and to continue receiving Pan-EU benefits. Additionally, starting 26 February 2027, all Pan-EU FBA products must also have an active offer in Belgium at enrolment. We'll notify you at least 30 days before this takes effect. The Pan-European FBA program includes additional store options: amazon.se, amazon.ie, and amazon.pl. Offers in these stores remain option[al]." (UK-PANEU.) The same in the News_Amazon post *Pan-EU FBA to require Netherlands and Belgium offer* (posted 2026-08-21), https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/61296686-3581-4d1a-b3c2-dacbef755b22.
  - History: the Netherlands offer was first required for *new* enrolments only: "New products enrolled in Pan-European Fulfilment by Amazon … must now have an offer in our Netherlands store, in addition to the existing requirement to have an offer in Germany, Spain, France and Italy." and "Later this year, you'll be required to list your existing Pan-EU Fulfilment by Amazon offers on Amazon.nl" (News_Amazon, *Netherlands offers now required for new Pan-EU Fulfilment by Amazon products*, posted 2025-08-28, https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/d9050e2c-8a52-4ebc-acc7-6cef81c77225).
  - Exemptions Amazon lists (same two posts): "Your products are restricted or can't be sold in the Netherlands or Belgium. Your products don't comply with local language labelling or packaging requirements. Your account is temporarily deactivated in the Netherlands or Belgium store. Your product doesn't have a product detail page in the Netherlands or Belgium store yet." The 2026 post says the exemption may be "for a limited or unlimited period".
  - Before Brexit the required stores included the UK: "List and sell your products in the required Amazon European stores (Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.de, Amazon.fr, Amazon.es and Amazon.it)" (News_Amazon, *Introducing the new Pan-European FBA programme*, posted 2020-10-29, https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/842e5ff8b29a72506322e42758725a41). That post also says "Amazon will also place your products in countries where you already have provided a VAT registration number in Seller Central."
- **Which fee a unit pays.** "Through Pan-European FBA, you will pay local fulfillment fees in all the EU countries enabled for storage, and Cross-border fulfilment (EFN) fees in the rest of the EU countries." (UK-PANEU.) Two store-specific conditions: "To enjoy domestic FBA Fulfilment fees for orders in Sweden and the Netherlands, you must enable placement in Germany. For orders in Belgium, you must enable placement in either Germany or France." The 2026 forum post words it "Netherlands: Local fees apply when inventory placement is active in Germany. Belgium: Local fees apply when inventory placement is active in Germany or France".
- **Fees that exist only for Pan-EU stock** (amounts in `areas/holding-costs.md`): the rate card's contents list a "Pan-EU Oversize Surcharge" and a "Pan-EU Low-Inventory Cost Coverage Fee" (RC-EU, page 10).
- **How Amazon says the movement is tracked.** "You can track the movement of your inventory using the Cross-Border Inventory Movement report, the Daily Inventory History report, and the Amazon Fulfilled Shipments report." (UK-PANEU.) What SP-API carries of this is section J3.
- **Eligibility is per ASIN and can lapse.** The report `GET_PAN_EU_OFFER_STATUS` (section J2) carries a `Pan-EU status`, an `Enrollment Date`, a `Last active on` and a `Date Pan-EU expires` column. The page that states the lapse rule (how long an offer may be inactive in a required store before Pan-EU status ends) is a Seller Central help page and was not reachable.
- **Where Amazon's pages disagree on Pan-EU.**
  - The US page *FBA international*, https://sell.amazon.com/fulfillment-by-amazon/fba-international (read the same day), still says "List products in our France, Germany, Italy, and Spain stores using the same SKUs" and "Pan-European FBA is available in France, Germany, Italy, Spain, and Poland", with no Netherlands requirement; UK-PANEU says "Pan-European FBA is available in the following Amazon stores: Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Poland, Sweden, Belgium and Ireland."
  - Inside UK-PANEU itself: step 3 names five storage countries (FR, DE, IT, ES, PL), while the FAQ says "you must have access to seller central accounts in the following EU stores: France, Germany, Italy, Spain and the Netherlands*. You must enable placement in at least two of these stores", which reads as if the Netherlands were a placement country.

### B. Europe without Pan-EU: EFN, the Central Europe Programme, Multi-Country Inventory, the UK, Ireland, compliance blocks

- **EFN.** One pool in one country serves orders from other EU stores at a cross-border fee. The rate card: "If you have a Seller Central account in an EU country outside of IE, the European Fulfilment Network enables you to fulfil orders placed on Amazon.IE from inventory stored in other EU countries paying IE EFN fee rates." and "We may review European Fulfilment Network fees and make adjustments with a 30-day notice when the foreign exchange rate change between the British pound and the euro exceeds 5% on a monthly, quarterly or yearly basis." (RC-EU, pages 16 and 12 notes) [official]. The Pan-EU handbook's comparison table sets EFN ("A VAT number is required" only where stock is stored; "EFN fee applies") against Pan-EU ("Local fees in all the countries enables for storage") [official; 2022 figures].
- **Central Europe Programme (CEP; older pages write CEE).** It is a condition on German stock, priced as a surcharge for not joining: "The fees will differ if you have FBA inventory in Germany and do not participate in the Central Europe Programme (CEP) by allowing Amazon to store your German FBA inventory in Poland and the Czech Republic. You are charged an additional £0.26, €0.26, SEK 2.50, or PLN 1.15 of fulfilment fee per FBA unit shipped from German fulfilment centres for both standard FBA and Low-Price FBA Rates. Units sold in Germany from Poland are charged the German domestic fee." (RC-EU, pages 5 to 7; the fee tables carry separate columns "CEP (DE/PL/CZ)" and "DE (non CEE)") [official]. The Czech Republic has no Amazon store; it is a storage country only.
- **Multi-Country Inventory (MCI).** The seller ships stock to chosen countries itself and Amazon does not rebalance it. The term appears in SP-API's description of `GET_AFN_INVENTORY_DATA_BY_COUNTRY`: "helping Multi-Country Inventory sellers in Europe track their FBA inventory" (section J1) [official]. No sell.amazon.* page read in 2026 uses the name MCI; UK-PANEU and the UK–EU page describe the same thing as storing "locally" in each country. The MCI help page was not reachable.
- **The UK after Brexit.** Source: *Sell across UK and EU borders*, https://sell.amazon.co.uk/fulfilment-by-amazon/fulfilment-between-uk-eu (UK-EU), and its German twin https://sell.amazon.de/en/versand-durch-amazon/verkaufe-zwischen-eu-uk [official].
  - Two routes: "Local Inbounding into UK and EU means shipping your inventory to both your primary (e.g. UK) and secondary (e.g. EU) FBA networks." "Remote Fulfillment allows you to sell across the UK-EU customs border without having to store inventory in the destination marketplace or register for VAT there. You simply send your FBA inventory to your local fulfillment centers, and Amazon will be responsible for collecting and remitting VAT when handling cross-border orders on your behalf."
  - Amazon's comparison table: importer of record "Seller" (local inbounding) against "Customer / Amazon" (Remote Fulfilment); warehouse "Destination" against "Source"; VAT number "VRN on destination store" against "Not required"; fees "Local fulfillment fees" against "Remote Fulfillment fees"; Prime badge "Yes" for both; "Price eligibility of ASINs: None" against "Must not exceed 135EUR/122GBP"; "Customer sees a product as domestic" against "Customers sees a product as an import".
  - Arcs: "You will be able to sell products on the arcs between the UK and Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Sweden and the Netherlands." Returns: "If a customer returns an imported item, we'll send it back to your source fulfillment center in the UK/EU, subject to customer returns policy and fees."
  - Enrolment path Amazon gives: "Inventory → Remote Fulfillment with FBA → Marketplace Enrolment tab".
  - The older handbook *EFN between the UK and the EU* (PDF, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/fba/Handbook_EFN_between_the_UK_and_the_EU_EN.pdf) calls the same route EFN and gives the tax line: "For business-to-consumer sales that do not exceed €150/£135, Amazon must account for VAT, which the customer pays at checkout. For such sales above €150/£135, and all business-to-business sales, the customer is the importer of record and pays any import VAT and duties." and the exclusions: "Which programmes are currently excluded from the European Fulfilment Network between the UK and the EU? … Hazmat, Small and Light, and Heavy & Bulky." [official; undated handbook, written when the route was reopened in 2022].
  - **Disagreement:** UK-EU (2026) caps Remote Fulfilment ASINs at "135EUR/122GBP" in its comparison table; the older handbook states no price cap and, in its VAT answer, describes B2C sales above "€150/£135" as possible with the customer as importer of record. The handbook's figure is a VAT threshold, the page's is an eligibility cap; no page read reconciles them (verifier, 2026-11-10).
  - Shipping stock across the border is the seller's import: "Amazon Customs Clearance and Shipping Services provides an all-in-one solution for the brokerage, indirect representation, and transportation services required to send cross-border shipments." (UK-EU.)
- **Northern Ireland.** After the transition period: "For the majority of FBA sellers, even after the grace period, Amazon will continue to send FBA orders to Northern Ireland. However, product categories, shipping exclusions to Northern Ireland will apply" (News_Amazon, *Brexit update: Shipping orders from the United Kingdom to Northern Ireland*, posted 2021-01-20, https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/fc51f7bdd67a001c216cd6b791bd1f3a) [official]. In 2026 Amazon's compliance hub says "GPSR (Northern Ireland): For sales to Northern Ireland or the EU, you must comply with the General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR), effective Dec 2024, which requires a designated EU contact person." (https://sell.amazon.co.uk/selling-partner-compliance-hub) [official]. So UK stock sold to a Northern Ireland address falls under an EU product rule.
- **Amazon.ie.** "Amazon.ie, our dedicated store for Ireland is officially live. Your existing product listings on Amazon.ie are now available to Irish customers" with "discounted FBA and Pan-European FBA fees when you use the local fulfilment centre" (News_Amazon, *Amazon.ie is now live in Ireland*, posted 2025-03-18, https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/49229ca0-78cc-468a-a446-761232100e3b) [official]. Rate card: "FBA in IE enables you to fulfil items in Ireland from inventory stored in Ireland paying IE Domestic/Pan-EU fee rates." and "The FBA fulfilment fee rates valid for Ireland are promotional" (RC-EU, page 16) [official]. Ireland is a store an EU account sells in; whether UK stock can serve it by Remote Fulfilment is not stated (the arcs list above does not name Ireland).
- **GPSR and EPR as reasons stock cannot sell.** "Products that lack the right compliance documentation, or aren't adhering to EPR standards, put your listings at risk of removal. And if non-compliant items are shipped internationally, they could be blocked at customs." (compliance hub, same URL) [official]. The pages that state the exact consequence per country (offer suppressed, stock stranded, Amazon paying EPR on the seller's behalf for a fee) are help pages and were not reachable; the listing playbook's file owns GPSR as a listing constraint. EPR's SP-API reports are in section J7.
- **GPSR and EPR: what happens to the offer and the stock (gap fill 2026-11-10).** Sources opened by the verifier's helper and spot-checked; forum dates from the page HTML.
  - GPSR took effect on 2024-12-13 for EU stores and Northern Ireland, not Great Britain: "For listings on Amazon UK-based store, you don't need to submit any information to Amazon. You must, however, submit the required information for listings on Amazon EU-based stores." and "Non-compliant listings risk removal" (Ezra_Amazon, *Important Announcement: General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) Implementation*, posted 2024-04-24, https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/611e88dd-bc36-4cdd-b74b-380416e8c28e); "The GPSR will not apply in Great Britain (England, Scotland and Wales). It will, however, apply in Northern Ireland" (Micah_Amazon, posted 2024-05-21) [official].
  - The act is on the offer: "If we become aware of noncompliant offers, such as those with invalid information or expired contracts, we will deactivate them." (News_Amazon, *Manage GPSR compliance with new detail page format*, posted 2024-09-10, https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/0bd7fc38-8d08-40a4-8407-0dd714b9d947) [official]. On FBA stock an Amazon moderator answered: "if you have inventory that is non-compliant, the solution is to remove them to avoid impacts to your selling abilities." (Roberto_Amazon, reply in *FBA stocks with no EU responsible person details*, thread posted 2024-10-31, https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/f4859edc-fcbb-4bb4-86c1-5dc296bd2afc) [official, forum staff]. A seller in the same thread held that goods placed on the market before 2024-12-13 are exempt; Amazon did not answer that [opinion, anonymous seller].
  - Germany, packaging EPR: "Amazon will start blocking non-compliant listings on June 15, 2022" without a validated LUCID number, and "It may take up to four weeks to register in addition to five working days for Amazon to validate your LUCID number." (News_Amazon, *ALERT: Effective June 15, 2022, your listings may be blocked for non-compliance with EPR packaging law in Germany*, posted 2022-06-13, https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/eae0ca6321720e594c8bb102ea9e07d2) [official, 2022].
  - France: "If you sell products on Amazon.fr and don't take the above steps, Amazon will automatically enrol you in the EPR Pay on Behalf service." and "If selling partnerss are considered non-compliant, Amazon may need to suspend their non-compliant offers." (*A Guide to Environmental Compliance for Amazon Selling Partners UK*, https://sell.amazon.co.uk/compliance-solutions/environmental) [official]; the service "reports and pays eco-contribution fees for your sales in Amazon stores, where available, and then debits those amounts from your selling account" (https://sell.amazon.fr/en/solutions-conformite/environnement) [official]. From 2025-08-27 for the UK, Spain and France: "We may hold EPR eco-contribution and service fees in reserve" (News_Amazon, *EPR Pay on Behalf policy update for UK, Spain and France*, posted 2025-07-28, https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/732822dc-4a85-429e-8d91-ea2ffa32d9e5) [official].
  - What no Amazon page read says: that GPSR- or EPR-deactivated offers' FBA units are "stranded" by that name, or a disposal deadline for them. The stated mechanism is offer deactivation or blocking, then a removal the seller orders. How such units then appear in the stock reads is owned by `areas/stuck-stock.md`.
- **A surcharge that touches every EU and UK FBA unit.** "Starting November 2, 2026, the fuel and logistics-related surcharge will increase from 1.5% to 2% and will apply to fulfillment fees across Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) in our UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Poland, Sweden, Netherlands, Ireland, and Belgium stores." (UK-PANEU, UK-EU, RC-EU) [official]. Amount owned by `areas/holding-costs.md`.

### C. North America: Remote Fulfillment with FBA, the unified account, and own stock in Canada, Mexico and Brazil

Sources: *Remote Fulfillment with FBA*, https://sell.amazon.com/fulfillment-by-amazon/remote-fulfillment (US-RF); *How to sell globally from the US*, https://sell.amazon.com/global-selling/usa-to-international (US-INTL); the country pages under https://sell.amazon.com/global-selling/; News_Amazon posts. All [official].

- **What the program is.** "Remote Fulfillment with FBA simplifies international sales by letting you sell to customers in Canada, Mexico, and Brazil without sending inventory to those countries. Create offers on Amazon.ca, Amazon.com.mx, and Amazon.com.br, and we'll use your US FBA inventory to deliver orders across the border with Prime shipping." (US-RF.) The launch post covered Canada and Mexico only: "Any offer you create on Amazon.ca or Amazon.com.mx automatically pulls from your US inventory, unless the item is already in a fulfillment center in Canada or Mexico." (News_Amazon, *Selling in Canada and Mexico just got easier*, posted 2020-09-29, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/448a6c37fd8ee1e58835f71c712fa126.)
  - That sentence is the only statement found of the precedence between local and remote stock: local stock in the destination country is used first.
- **Who can use it.** "make sure you have a Professional selling plan, a North America and Brazil unified selling account, and are enrolled in FBA in the US with FBA Export enabled." (US-RF.)
- **Importer and tax.** "Customers pay any import duties, taxes, and fees as the importer of record. That means you don't need to pay taxes in Canada, Mexico, or Brazil." (US-RF.)
- **Fees.** "sellers will pay the Remote Fulfillment with FBA costs and the category referral fee for the store where the sale occurred. These costs replace US FBA costs and referral fees." (US-RF.) The holiday peak fulfilment fee applies to Remote Fulfillment with FBA (section I).
- **Eligibility.** "To be eligible, a product must: Be enabled for FBA in the US and have valid offers in Canada, Mexico, or Brazil; Meet all trade compliance and destination-country regulations. The following product types are not eligible: Restricted products: Canada, Mexico, Brazil; Prohibited products: Canada, Mexico, Brazil; Dangerous goods: Canada, Mexico, Brazil; Blocked for importation: Mexico, Brazil". "Once enrolled, you can check product eligibility through your ASIN Eligibility Report. Download this report from the Remote Fulfillment with FBA section in Seller Central … you can also manually enroll or unenroll products by typing "Yes" or "No" in the "Enable/Disable" column." (US-RF.) SP-API's twin is `GET_REMOTE_FULFILLMENT_ELIGIBILITY` (section J4).
- **Returns.** "Returns will be sent back to the US and are subject to the FBA customer returns policy." (US-RF.)
- **SKU rule.** "While enrolled in Remote Fulfillment with FBA, seller-fulfilled offers can't use the same SKUs as FBA offers. To maintain seller-fulfilled offers for the same items, assign the seller-fulfilled offer a different SKU." (US-RF.)
- **Listings and price.** The Build International Listings tool "replicates your eligible US offers and lists them in the destination stores" and "Any manual price adjustments in the destination store will break the connection to the Build International Listings tool and disable the automatic pricing rule." (US-RF.)
- **Brazil.** "Eligible North America Unified Accounts can now expand listings to Brazil and start selling seller-fulfilled orders or remote fulfillment with FBA. If eligible, you'll see Brazil as an option in the store switcher at the top of Seller Central." and "Note: Selling with FBA in Brazil is only available to accounts with Brazil listed as their reporting country, or accounts that signed up on Amazon.com.br." (News_Amazon, *Eligible North America Unified Accounts can now expand to Brazil*, posted 2024-04-19, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/b50bb8c2-1b83-463b-857c-ce3783672a8d.) Local FBA in Brazil needs a Brazilian company: "disponível para vendedores parceiros com conta registrada na Amazon.com.br com CNPJ" with a state registration in one of "SP, MG, PR, RJ, SC, RS, CE, DF, PE, BA, GO ou ES" (https://venda.amazon.com.br/cresca/fba; gloss: FBA is for accounts registered on Amazon.com.br with a CNPJ and a state tax registration in one of twelve states). Brazil also has "DBA - Delivery by Amazon" ("Você vende e embala, a Amazon entrega": the seller holds and packs, Amazon delivers; https://venda.amazon.com.br/cresca/dba) and "FBA Onsite" in its menu.
- **The unified account.** "There is a single Professional selling plan fee for North America and Brazil unified accounts, allowing you to sell in the US, Mexico, Brazil, and Canada for USD 39.99 per month." (https://sell.amazon.com/global-selling/latin-america.) The account is one login over four stores; stock sent to a Canadian or Mexican fulfilment center is a separate pool that the seller imports: "When sending inventory to Amazon fulfillment centers in other countries, you or your provider must act as the exporter of record and importer of record and must comply with all laws and regulations in both countries." (US-INTL, "FBA worldwide".)
- **Three routes from US stock, as Amazon lists them** (US-INTL): "FBA Export" (the customer shops on Amazon.com from abroad and "they'll see a price that includes international shipping and an import fee"), "Remote Fulfillment with FBA" (the customer shops on Amazon.ca, .com.mx or .com.br), and "FBA worldwide" ("exporting your inventory to Amazon fulfillment centers in those countries").
- **Where Amazon's pages disagree on NARF.** Amazon Global Selling China's page still describes two countries: "美国站点卖家可以向加拿大和墨西哥买家销售商品，而无需将库存运入这两个国家境内" (gloss: US-store sellers can sell to buyers in Canada and Mexico without moving stock into those two countries) and names the report "ASIN 状态报告" (ASIN status report) where US-RF says "ASIN Eligibility Report" (https://globalselling.amazon.com/logistics/narf) [official]. The Holiday 2026 post links "Remote Fulfillment by FBA fee rates for Mexico and Canada" and names no Brazil rate page (section I).

### D. Other stores: what Amazon's pages say about stock

- **Separate accounts, separate pools.** Japan, Australia, India, the UAE and Saudi Arabia each have their own selling account and their own FBA network; none shares stock with the US or with each other. "There are separate Professional selling plan fees for a Japan account and North America and Brazil unified account. However, if you link global Amazon selling accounts, your monthly fee will be either the equivalent of USD 39.99 per month or the sum of the selling plan fees for each region and country in which you have active listings, whichever is lower." (https://sell.amazon.com/global-selling/japan; the Australia, India and Middle East pages carry the same sentence with their store named; the Middle East page says "separate Professional selling plan fees for UAE, Saudi Arabia, and North America and Brazil unified accounts") [official].
- **Japan.** "Amazon's Japan Multichannel Fulfillment (MCF) service helps you grow your Japan-based business both in and beyond the Amazon store." (same page) [official]. **E1. What it is.** "Amazon Warehousing and Distribution (AWD) is a bulk storage solution that distributes your inventory to the Amazon store and non-Amazon sales channels. AWD pricing covers FBA inbound placement, so there is no separate charge for this service. AWD's auto-replenishment capability ensures your FBA inventory is kept in stock." (US-AWD.) Stock in AWD is upstream of FBA and is a separate pool: "Can I send inventory currently stored in FBA to AWD instead? No. You can't move inventory from FBA to AWD because items are stored differently in our fulfillment and distribution centers." (US-AWD.) "When you sign up for FBA, you get immediate access to MCF, AWD, and MCD." (US-ASCS.) Amazon China now calls it "亚马逊智能卫星仓(AWD)" (smart satellite warehouse); older pages on the same site still say "亚马逊入仓分销网络 (AWD)".

**E4. Replenishment from AWD to FBA.**
- Three settings per SKU: "FBA auto-replenishment with Amazon recommended quantity: Amazon determines how much inventory is needed in FBA and automatically creates shipments and replenishes on your behalf. / FBA auto-replenishment with quantity limit: You specify the minimum and maximum units that FBA will hold, and AWD replenishes up to that amount. / Turn off FBA auto-replenishment: You manually control inventory movement from AWD to FBA." And: "for products with multiple seller SKUs under the same ASIN, Amazon uses the highest value set across all SKUs to determine replenishment quantity. Updates to these settings can take up to 24 hours to take effect." (US-BLOG-AWD.)
- How Amazon decides: "Auto-replenishment relies on a proprietary data-science model that monitors the inventory levels in Prime-ready fulfillment centers and triggers replenishments in order to maintain adequate inventory levels so that your products stay in stock." (US-AWD.) "系统会在两个时间点计算补货数量： ►每周根据销量和亚马逊运营中心的库存情况，计算补货建议； ►每天计算来避免销量临时激增导致亚马逊运营中心缺货。" (quantities are computed weekly from sales and fulfilment-center stock, and daily to catch a surge; CN-AWD.) For GWD's version of the same feature Amazon publishes the model: a 53-week demand forecast including seasonality and registered deals, plus lead time, sets a target inventory position per SKU, and the gap is ordered when FBA on-hand plus in-transit is below it; whole cases only, rounding at half a case; "自动补货不是实时触发；没有订单不一定表示出现问题" (not real-time; no order does not mean a fault) (CN-GWD-AR `/what-ar`, `/supply-ar`). Whether US AWD uses the same logic is not stated on a US page.
- Capacity limits: "If you leave auto-replenishment enabled, your products are considered in stock and buyable when they're received by AWD, and we manage your replenishments so you don't have to worry about Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) capacity limits." (US-AWD.) "AWD auto-replenished inventory is NOT subject to FBA capacity limits" (F-Q4). Manual replenishment is inside them: "由于手动补货需遵守亚马逊物流容量限制，因此如果创建的货件超出限制，请求的库存补货将被拒绝。" (manual replenishment obeys FBA capacity limits; a shipment over the limit is rejected; CN-GWD-AR `/what-ar`.)
- Buyable before it reaches a fulfilment center, with a slower promise: "当运营中心缺货时，在亚马逊AWD中的ASIN或从AWD运输到亚马逊运营中心途中的 ASIN可以被搜索或购买" (when the fulfilment center is out, an ASIN in AWD or in transit from AWD can be found and bought; CN-AWD); under manual replenishment only when stock is in transit (CN-GWD-AR). "the promised delivery dates for units in AWD facilities (or in transit from AWD to a fulfillment center) will differ from delivery dates for units that are already at a fulfillment center." (US-ASCS.)
- Lead time Amazon has stated: "库存被运送到亚马逊AWD仓库后，亚马逊将在7-14天的运输时效内处理到亚马逊运营中心的补货" (once stock is in AWD, Amazon handles replenishment to the fulfilment center within a 7-to-14-day transit time; https://gs.amazon.cn/logistics/mcd, undated; the English wording the seat quoted was not found on the page re-read by the verifier).
- Over-sending by hand is charged in FBA: "当卖家由AWD向亚马逊运营中心手动补货过量时，存放在亚马逊运营中心的超期库存将按照亚马逊物流超龄库存附加费的政策收取费用" (stock over-replenished by hand pays the FBA aged-inventory surcharge; CN-AWD).

**E5. The benefits Amazon attaches, each with its condition.**
- Inbound placement fee: not charged separately on AWD stock (US-AWD, above). Amazon's GWD guide says a minimal-split placement chosen by auto-replenishment does carry the placement fee and is chosen only when total cost is lowest (CN-GWD-AR `/fee-ar`); whether that applies to US AWD is not stated.
- Low-inventory-level fee and storage utilization surcharge: waived for a SKU when 70% or more of its inbound over 90 days was auto-replenished. "the low-inventory-level and storage utilization fees don't apply to that SKU, so long as you auto-replenished 70% or more of that SKU to FBA through AWD over the previous 90 days." and "If you use AWD auto-replenishment but limit the maximum number of units of a SKU that can be auto-replenished to FBA, the low-inventory-level fee will apply to that SKU if the inventory level falls below the required days of supply in FBA." (help page *AWD fees*, GAYG62Q3MPE6STFS) [snippet]. The ratio as Amazon defines it: "自动补货比例 = 自动补货发货量 ÷（自动补货 + 手动补货 + 直接入库 FBA 的发货量）。系统每天按过去 90 天的发货量计算；该 ASIN 的 90 天计算从首个自动补货货件创建之日开始。" (auto-replenished units ÷ (auto + manual + direct-to-FBA units), recomputed daily over 90 days starting at the first auto shipment; CN-GWD-AR `/what-ar`), which also lists a third waiver, "181–365天库龄商品的FBA超龄库存附加费" (the FBA aged-inventory surcharge for stock aged 181 to 365 days).
- Storage discounts (US-AWD card; amounts in `areas/holding-costs.md`): a "Smart storage rate" ("10% off base rate") and an "Amazon managed rate" ("20% off base rate" on storage, "10% off base rate" on transportation). Conditions (CN-AWD): smart storage needs the 70% auto ratio over 90 days and "该SKU 在AWD和FBA中的库存水平必须等于或大于70天" (the SKU's stock across AWD and FBA is 70 days of supply or more); the managed rate needs both plus AGL, Amazon SEND or PCP as the inbound carrier. Created 2025-04-01 (F-2025FEES).
- No peak pricing in 2026: "AWD has simple, pay-as-you-go pricing with no additional costs for the holiday season." (US-AWD.) For 2026 only: "if you send inventory through AWD and enable automatic replenishment to FBA, you'll keep paying the off-peak monthly storage rate through October 31, 2026." (F-HOL26.)
- IPI: "卖家的IPI不会受到库存在AWD的停留时间的影响，只有在库存转移到FBA运营中心后才会受到影响" (time in AWD does not affect IPI; only once stock moves to FBA; CN-AWD).
- Amazon's result claim: "Sellers who enrolled in AWD in the fourth quarter of 2025 experienced an increase of over 13% in shipped units and greater than 30% reduction in out-of-stock days during the quarter." (F-HOL26.)

(SP-2027.) For AWD: from 2027-03-01 storage will "vary based on inventory age, with inventory stored for more than 12 months charged at 2.5 times the standard rate"; Amazon will introduce storage reservations "to make capacity more predictable" and, in PPC Land's own words, "removal and disposal options with corresponding fees"; the fuel and logistics surcharge reaches AWD transportation from 2027-02-01 [official, via PPC Land; the removal and disposal clause is PPC Land's paraphrase]. ### H. Special-rule stock

**H1. Dangerous goods (hazmat).**
- A product under classification review cannot be sold or inbounded; the API's reason codes give the wording and the review times (section J9).
- Storage for flammable and aerosol goods is its own allocation, not set by IPI: "Beginning 03/31/2022, DG Flammable and/or Aerosol storage increase requests were no longer being accepted. Going forward, storage allocation will be based on Seller performance, and how quickly units inbounded are shipped and sold. Sellers must maintain a balanced DG inventory level between sold and on-hand DG inventory to receive an increase in storage capacity. Selling Partners who have unbalanced ratios of Storage to Selling will receive reductions in storage capacity. … we will regularly assess active utilization and marketplace sales, and automatically adjust allocation of DG Flammable and/or Aerosol storage limits per FBA Dangerous Goods program Terms and Conditions; not Inventory Performance Index (IPI) scores." (Kelly_Amazon, *Ask Amazon: FBA Dangerous Goods Program Storage Capacity*, posted 2022-04-26, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/abd42500460538df0e71705be3e0ca66) [official; US; a 2022 statement, not re-confirmed for 2026].
- "Hazardous materials are stored in specially designed safety areas to minimize risks." (*What Amazon FBA sellers should know about dangerous goods (hazmat)*, https://sell.amazon.com/blog/amazon-hazmat-dangerous-goods) [official].
- Excluded from programs: Remote Fulfillment with FBA to Canada, Mexico and Brazil (section C); Remote Fulfilment between the UK and the EU (section B, handbook); AWD except the listed batteries (E3). Dangerous goods carry their own per-unit fee on the EU rate card: "a per-unit fee of £0.10, €0.10, SEK 1.00 and PLN 0.40 for products that contain or are sold with lithium batteries and for dangerous goods (also known as hazmat) is applied to cover the additional costs associated with processing these products" (RC-EU; amounts owned by `areas/holding-costs.md`) [official].

**H3. Expiry-dated and lot-controlled stock.**
- "Amazon considers any product with a shelf life, or that loses effectiveness with time, to be subject to expiration, even if an expiration date is not indicated on the product packaging. When your items arrive at a fulfillment center, they must have a remaining shelf life that allows time for the product to be consumed/used with an additional 90 days. Perishable items are not allowed." (TaylorR_Amazon, *FBA and Expiration Dates: Here's what you need to know*, posted 2023-11-22, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/5eafed1d-476b-4920-82b6-f7709c148e80) [official; US].

- Removal from sale: "Any inventory that exceeds the expiration date or that has an expiration date that cannot be confirmed will be marked as unsellable and you will need to remove it from the fulfillment center." ### L. The Replenishment API is Subscribe & Save (v2022-11-07)

Sources: *Replenishment API* (https://developer-docs.amazon.com/sp-api/docs/replenishment-api, page stamp 2026-09-09), *Role Mappings for SP-API Operations*, *SP-API Release Notes*, *SP-API Deprecation Schedule*, local model `models/replenishment-api-model/replenishment-2022-11-07.json`. All [official], read 2026-11-10.

- **What it is.** "Currently, the Subscribe & Save replenishment program is supported." It returns "metrics about a selling partner's replenishment business performance and information about their replenishment program offers". It says nothing about FBA restock quantities.
- **Where.** "The Replenishment API is available wherever Amazon Subscribe & Save is live. The API is also available to vendors and Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) selling partners." The model's `marketplaceId` description narrows it: "The supported marketplaces for both sellers and vendors are US, CA, ES, UK, FR, IT, IN, DE, and JP. The supported marketplaces for vendors only are BR, AU, MX, AE, and NL."
- **Role.** `getSellingPartnerMetrics`, `listOfferMetrics`, `listOffers`: **Brand Analytics or Inventory and Order Tracking**; regions NA, EU, FE. Brand Analytics is among the five. All three are POST searches, 1 request per second, burst 1.
- **Stock fields on an offer** (`listOffers`, enriched by the release note of 2026-04-01: "support inventory and delivery health filtering, sorting, and enriched offer details … filter offers by delivery condition (paused, at-risk, or healthy), sort by inventory or price, and monitor stock levels and delivery health"):
  - `inventory`: "The available inventory count for the offer."
  - `stockRisk`: "The stock risk level of the offer, indicating the risk of the offer going out of stock." A string; the model gives no list of values.
  - `deliveriesConditions[]`: a `condition` and `next30DaysDeliveries` ("The number of upcoming deliveries in the next 30 days associated with this delivery condition."). Conditions: `NEXT_30_DAYS_DELIVERIES_PAUSED_PRICING`, `NEXT_30_DAYS_DELIVERIES_PAUSED_NON_BUYABLE`, `NEXT_30_DAYS_DELIVERIES_AT_LOW_INVENTORY_RISK_ONLY`, `NEXT_30_DAYS_DELIVERIES_AT_LOW_INVENTORY_RISK`, `NO_ISSUES_FOR_NEXT_30_DAYS_DELIVERIES`.
  - `forecastDeliveries`: `next15DaysDeliveries`, `next30DaysDeliveries`, `next60DaysDeliveries`, `next90DaysDeliveries`, each "The projected number of subscriber deliveries".
  - `subscriptions`: "The number of active subscriptions for the offer."
  - `eligibility`: `ELIGIBLE` ("able to fulfill current subscriptions and add new subscriptions"); `INELIGIBLE` ("will not be able to add new subscriptions and existing subscriptions will be cancelled"); `SUSPENDED` ("will not be able to add new subscriptions but existing subscriptions will be fulfilled"); `REPLENISHMENT_ONLY_ORDERING` ("will not be able to add new subscriptions but existing subscriptions will be fulfilled. This eligibility status also blocks one-time purchases to preserve inventory for existing subscriptions.").
  - `offerProgramConfiguration`: `enrollmentMethod` `MANUAL` or `AUTOMATIC`; `preferences.autoEnrollment` `OPTED_IN` or `OPTED_OUT`; the four discounts `sellingPartnerFundedBaseDiscount`, `sellingPartnerFundedTieredDiscount`, `amazonFundedBaseDiscount`, `amazonFundedTieredDiscount`.
  - `fulfillmentNetworkIDType`: "indicating how the offer is fulfilled".
- **Metrics that are about stock.** `NOT_DELIVERED_DUE_TO_OOS`: "The percentage of items that were not shipped out of the total shipped units over a period of time due to being out of stock." `LOST_REVENUE_DUE_TO_OOS`: "The revenue that would have been generated had there not been out of stock." (added 2024-06-19). Both exist per seller (`getSellingPartnerMetrics`) and per offer (`listOfferMetrics`; since 2026-04-01 "at the offer level instead of the ASIN level", with `sku` and `fulfillmentChannelType` `AMAZON` or `MERCHANT`).
- **Forecast.** `timePeriodType` is `PERFORMANCE` or `FORECAST`: "Indicates forecasted metrics. Only `TOTAL_SUBSCRIPTIONS_REVENUE` and `SHIPPED_SUBSCRIPTION_UNITS` are supported. - **The reports are gone.** `GET_FBA_SNS_FORECAST_DATA` ("an eight-week forecast of sales based on existing subscriptions and sales history") and `GET_FBA_SNS_PERFORMANCE_DATA` ("a trailing four-week view of items shipped, percentage of orders missed due to being out of stock, average post-discount sale price, and average discount for each SKU") were launched in the release note of 2022-10-19, deprecated on 2025-06-25 and removed on 2025-12-11 (deprecation schedule; release notes of 2025-06-25 and 2025-08-27: "The following report type values will be removed on December 11, 2025"). **Amazon's pages disagree:** *Role Mappings for SP-API Types* (page stamp 2026-09-09) still lists both with role Amazon Fulfillment; the FBA Reports page (2026-10-13) no longer lists either.

## Stable enough for a skill

These are structural and have held across the pages and years read. Dates, figures and lists that move are in the next section.

1. Where a unit sits and which store's order it can serve are two different questions. Pan-EU, the Central Europe Programme, EFN, Remote Fulfilment (UK–EU), Remote Fulfillment with FBA (US to CA, MX, BR) and FBA Export each let one pool serve another store's or country's customers.
2. In Pan-EU and the Central Europe Programme Amazon moves stock between countries at its own discretion; under Multi-Country Inventory and local inbounding the seller places it. Storing in a country creates VAT reporting there (Amazon's own sentence, section A).
3. Pan-EU status is per ASIN, depends on live offers in a required list of stores, and can lapse; the required list has changed three times (2020 UK removed, NL added for new then all, BE dated 2027).
4. The fee a European unit pays depends on whether the country of the order is enabled for storage (local fee) or not (EFN fee), with store-specific conditions for SE, NL and BE.
5. The UK and the EU are two FBA networks with a customs border. Stock on one side reaches the other side's customers only through Remote Fulfilment (price-capped, customer or Amazon as importer) or by the seller importing stock.
6. Remote Fulfillment with FBA uses US stock, makes the customer the importer, returns units to the US, excludes dangerous goods, and gives way to local stock in the destination country.
7. Japan, Australia, India, the UAE and Saudi Arabia are separate accounts with separate pools. Brazil's local FBA needs a Brazilian company.
8. AWD is a separate upstream pool, one-way into FBA, with replenishment either decided by Amazon or by the seller; Amazon ties fee waivers and capacity-limit relief to the automatic mode, and each waiver has its own test.
9. AWD stock can make an offer buyable before the unit reaches a fulfilment center, with a later delivery promise.
10. AWD inbound and FBA inbound are different shipments to different buildings and cannot share a truck.
11. Special-rule stock is special in the same few ways: a calendar window (meltables), a remaining-life test (expiry), a separate storage allocation (flammable, aerosol, extra-large), a review that freezes stock (dangerous goods), exclusion from cross-border programs and from AWD.
12. From 2026-03-31 whether a unit needs an Amazon barcode depends on whether the seller is the brand's Brand Representative, not on whether a manufacturer barcode exists.
13. Each year Amazon publishes arrival cut-offs for each sales event, by shipment-split option and separately for AWD; receiving capacity turns to outbound work in November and December; a peak fulfilment fee runs mid-October to mid-January; returns of November and December orders run to the end of January.
14. The Replenishment API is Subscribe & Save, not restock; an out-of-stock offer loses deliveries and, by eligibility state, may lose subscriptions.
15. Amazon's pages on these programs lag each other; in every disagreement read on 2026-11-10 the store's own page or the newest dated announcement was more current than a US or China overview page (section N).

## Positions

Each is a fact or a discipline, with its marketplace scope; sources are Amazon's first, then the section.

- **P-programs-and-regions-1.** In Pan-European FBA (stores DE, FR, IT, ES, NL, PL, SE, BE, IE) the seller enables countries for storage and Amazon moves stock between them "at its own discretion and at any time"; where a unit sits is Amazon's decision, not the seller's. Source: sell.amazon.co.uk *Pan-European FBA* [official]; section A.
- **P-programs-and-regions-2.** Pan-EU status is per ASIN and depends on active offers in a required list of stores, which on 2026-11-10 is DE, FR, IT, ES and NL (NL for all products from 2026-09-03), with BE required at enrolment from 2027-02-26; the list is a dated value of Amazon's that has changed before (2020, 2025, 2026). Source: sell.amazon.co.uk *Pan-European FBA*; News_Amazon *Pan-EU FBA to require Netherlands and Belgium offer* [official]; section A.
- **P-programs-and-regions-3.** A European FBA unit pays the local fulfilment fee where the order's country is enabled for storage and the EFN fee elsewhere; local fees in SE and NL need placement in DE, and in BE need placement in DE or FR. Source: sell.amazon.co.uk *Pan-European FBA* [official]; section A.
- **P-programs-and-regions-4.** Storing stock in an EU country other than the home store creates VAT reporting there; Amazon states this on the Pan-EU page, and it applies to the Central Europe Programme's Poland and Czech Republic as well by practitioners' reading. Source: sell.amazon.co.uk *Pan-European FBA* [official]; hellotax 2026 [opinion]; sections A, B, P2.
- **P-programs-and-regions-5.** The Central Europe Programme is a condition on German FBA stock: a seller with stock in Germany who does not allow storage in Poland and the Czech Republic pays a per-unit surcharge on units shipped from German fulfilment centers; the amount is on the rate card in force (2026-11-02 edition). Source: *Europe Fees* rate card effective 2026-11-02 [official]; section B.
- **P-programs-and-regions-6.** In Europe, sellable stock by country is `GET_AFN_INVENTORY_DATA_BY_COUNTRY` (role Pricing or Amazon Fulfillment), and movement between countries is read from the inventory ledger reports (role Amazon Fulfillment); the Seller Central "Cross-Border Inventory Movement report" has no documented SP-API report type. Source: SP-API *Inventory Reports*, *FBA Reports*, *Role Mappings for SP-API Types* [official]; sections J1, J3.
- **P-programs-and-regions-7.** Pan-EU enrolment, per-store offer status and the date Pan-EU status expires are read per SKU from `GET_PAN_EU_OFFER_STATUS` (role Product Listing, Pricing or Amazon Fulfillment; also Inventory and Order Tracking or Direct to Consumer Shipping); its layout still carries UK and Small-and-Light columns that are not statements about the 2026 program. Source: SP-API *Inventory Reports*; *Role Mappings for SP-API Types* [official]; section J2.
- **P-programs-and-regions-8.** The UK and the EU are separate FBA networks: UK stock serves EU customers (and the reverse) only through Remote Fulfilment, which is price-capped, makes the customer or Amazon the importer and shows the product as an import, or through stock the seller imports and stores on the other side. Source: sell.amazon.co.uk *Sell across UK and EU borders* [official]; section B.
- **P-programs-and-regions-9.** Amazon's 2026 UK–EU program page caps Remote Fulfilment ASINs at "135EUR/122GBP"; its older handbook states no cap and treats "€150/£135" as the VAT threshold above which the customer is importer of record; no Amazon page read reconciles the two. Source: sell.amazon.co.uk UK–EU page; EFN UK–EU handbook PDF [official]; sections B, N.
- **P-programs-and-regions-10.** Dangerous goods are excluded from every cross-border remote program read: Remote Fulfilment UK–EU, Remote Fulfillment with FBA to CA, MX and BR, and AWD (Amazon China's AWD page admits listed lithium batteries from April 2026; the US AWD blog says lithium-ion batteries are not supported). Source: EFN UK–EU handbook; sell.amazon.com *Remote Fulfillment with FBA*; gs.amazon.cn AWD; sell.amazon.com AWD blog [official]; sections B, C, E3, H1.
- **P-programs-and-regions-11.** Remote Fulfillment with FBA sells US FBA stock on Amazon.ca, Amazon.com.mx and Amazon.com.br with the customer as importer of record; returns go back to US stock, and the fees replace US FBA fees and use the referral fee of the store of sale. Scope: North America and Brazil unified accounts with US FBA and FBA Export enabled. Source: sell.amazon.com *Remote Fulfillment with FBA* [official]; section C.
- **P-programs-and-regions-12.** An offer on Amazon.ca or Amazon.com.mx draws on US stock "unless the item is already in a fulfillment center in Canada or Mexico"; local stock comes first. This is Amazon's statement of 2020-09-29 and the only one found. Source: News_Amazon *Selling in Canada and Mexico just got easier* [official]; section C.
- **P-programs-and-regions-13.** NARF eligibility is per ASIN, changes over time, and is read from `GET_REMOTE_FULFILLMENT_ELIGIBILITY` (role Amazon Fulfillment), which has no destination-store column. Source: SP-API *FBA Reports* [official]; section J4.
- **P-programs-and-regions-14.** While enrolled in Remote Fulfillment with FBA, a seller-fulfilled offer cannot share a SKU with an FBA offer. Scope: US, CA, MX, BR. Source: sell.amazon.com *Remote Fulfillment with FBA* [official]; section C.
